Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

At AspirantDaily, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to sign up, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number through the site. Data is collected in these ways:

  • Browser storage: we use localStorage on your device to save quiz progress, scores and the day's questions for offline use.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Google collects usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser, and how you reached the site. We see this only as aggregated reports.
  • Google AdSense: Google and its advertising partners collect data, including cookies and device identifiers, to show and measure ads.
  • Email: if you write to us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the quiz experience and remember your progress on your device
  • To cache content for faster loading and offline use
  • To improve the website based on aggregated usage data
  • To show ads, which keep the site free

3. Cookies and Advertising

  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to count visits and sessions.
  • Advertising cookies: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the quizzes and notes still work without them.

4. Data Security

Quiz data stored in your browser (localStorage) remains on your device and is not sent to our servers. Data collected by Google Analytics and Google AdSense is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The latest version will always be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@teachings.ai
