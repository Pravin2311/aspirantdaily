SBI PO Exam Pattern 2026: Prelims, Mains, Descriptive Test, Interview and Final Merit

1,500 vacancies • Age and graduation • Number of chances • Prelims • Mains objective and descriptive • Phase-III • How the final merit is normalised

Banking 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01

State Bank of India recruits its Probationary Officers directly, not through IBPS. Advertisement No. CRPD/PO/2026-27/09 offered 1,500 vacancies, with online registration from 18 June to 8 July 2026. Its tentative schedule placed the prelims in August 2026, the mains in September 2026 and Phase-III in October or November 2026.

By category, the 1,500 posts are SC 234, ST 144, OBC 390, EWS 144 and UR 588.

Candidates must be at least 21 and at most 30 on 1 April 2026, so born between 2 April 1996 and 1 April 2005. Reserved categories get relaxations in the upper limit.

A degree in any discipline is required by 30 September 2026. Final-year students may apply provisionally but must show they passed by that date if called for interview. Professional qualifications such as medicine, engineering, chartered accountancy and cost accountancy also count.

SBI caps the number of attempts. Only appearing in the main examination counts as a chance.

The advertisement gives a total of 100 marks rather than section-wise marks. There is no sectional cut-off in the prelims: SBI draws a category-wise merit list on the aggregate and calls about ten times the vacancies in each category to the mains.

The mains is online and has two parts taken back to back: an objective test and a descriptive test typed on the computer.

The descriptive test has three tasks, each with a choice of two: an email, a situation analysis, and either a report or a precis.

Every one of the five parts has its own qualifying score, set by the Bank according to vacancies. Candidates also need a minimum aggregate, and about three times the category-wise vacancies go through to Phase-III.

Negative marking

In the objective tests of both prelims and mains, a wrong answer costs a quarter of the marks of that question. Unanswered questions cost nothing. In mains Test I, for instance, each question is worth 1.5 marks, so a wrong answer costs 0.375.

Phase-III starts with a psychometric test for personality profiling. Its findings are placed before the interview panel. Then come a group exercise worth 20 marks and an interview worth 30, a total of 50. The Bank sets the qualifying marks.

Candidates must qualify in Phase-II and Phase-III separately. The final merit list adds the mains (objective and descriptive, out of 230) and Phase-III (out of 50), after normalising them to 75 and 25 marks out of 100. Prelims marks are not added.

Worked example: the 75:25 conversion

A candidate scores 161 out of 230 in the mains and 33 out of 50 in Phase-III. Converted: 161 ÷ 230 × 75 = 52.5 and 33 ÷ 50 × 25 = 16.5, so the final score is 69 out of 100.

Selected candidates join as Probationary Officers in Junior Management Grade Scale-I, sign a ₹2 lakh bond to serve at least three years, and may be on probation for two years.

Are prelims marks counted in the SBI PO final merit?

No. The advertisement says prelims marks are not added to the final merit list. The prelims only shortlists about ten times the vacancies, on aggregate marks with no sectional cut-off.

How many attempts are allowed for SBI PO?

Six for UR and EWS candidates, nine for OBC and for UR or EWS candidates with benchmark disabilities, and no limit for SC and ST candidates. Only appearing in the main examination counts as an attempt.

How is SBI PO different from IBPS PO?

SBI runs its own recruitment. Its mains has a 30-mark descriptive test on emails, situation analysis and report or precis writing, Phase-III adds a group exercise to the interview, and the final merit is weighted 75:25 rather than IBPS's 80:20. See the IBPS PO page for that exam's pattern.
