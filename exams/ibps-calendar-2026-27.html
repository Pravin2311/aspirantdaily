IBPS Calendar 2026-27 (Tentative): Prelims and Mains Dates for PO, SO, Clerk and RRB Exams

PSB exams: PO/MT-XVI, SPL-XVI, CSA-XVI • RRB exams: CRP RRBs-XV Officers and Office Assistants • Cross-check with notifications • Upload sizes • Update log

Banking 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) publishes a tentative calendar each year for its common recruitment processes (CRPs) for public sector banks (PSBs) and regional rural banks (RRBs). The 2026-27 calendar is dated 16 January 2026. Every date in it is tentative: IBPS reserves the right to change exam dates and the selection process for administrative reasons, court orders or government advisories, so treat each exam's detailed notification and your call letter as final.

The status column below says only whether the calendar date has passed as of 1 November 2026. It is not a confirmation that the exam was held on that day.

The PO/MT-XVI notification gives the prelims as August 2026 and the mains as October 2026, and the CSA-XVI notification gives October and December 2026, which matches the calendar. A corrigendum to the CSA notification on 29 September 2026 updated only the indicative vacancies and left every other term unchanged.

For the syllabus and marks of these exams, see the IBPS PO exam pattern and the IBPS Clerk exam pattern.

Officer Scale II and III candidates take only one exam. Officer Scale I and Office Assistant candidates take a prelims first, and only those shortlisted go on to the main exam. The Office Assistant mains in January 2027 is the last stage in this calendar.

Registration for every exam is online only. Where an exam has both a prelims and a mains, one registration covers both. The calendar lists the files you must upload, and the specifications are worth checking before you start, because a rejected upload can hold up the form.

You must also capture a live photograph during the application, by webcam or mobile phone. The signature must be clearly visible and must not be in capital letters.

  • Plan backwards from the mains. Prelims shortlist you; the mains decides merit for PO and clerk posts. For the RRB Office Assistant track, the last prelims day (13 December) is 48 days, about seven weeks, before the 30 January mains.
  • Watch for clashes. The calendar puts the RRB Officer Scale I main exam and the Scale II/III single exam on the same day, 20 December 2026, so you cannot sit both on that date.
  • Confirm before you travel. Your call letter states the actual date, shift and centre.

When is the IBPS RRB Office Assistant exam in 2026?

The tentative calendar gives the prelims as 6, 12 and 13 December 2026 and the mains as 30 January 2027. Check your call letter and ibps.in for the confirmed dates.

Are the IBPS calendar dates final?

No. The calendar is tentative and IBPS says it may change dates for administrative reasons, court orders or government advisories. The detailed notification and call letter for each exam are the final word.

Is there a prelims for RRB Officer Scale II and III?

No. The calendar marks prelims as not applicable and lists a single exam on 20 December 2026.
