IBPS PO Exam Pattern 2026 (CRP PO/MT-XVI): Prelims, Mains, Interview and Eligibility

Participating banks and pay • Age and graduation • Prelims pattern • Mains pattern and descriptive paper • Personality test and interview • Final merit

Banking 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01

IBPS runs a Common Recruitment Process (CRP) for Probationary Officers and Management Trainees in public sector banks. The sixteenth round, CRP PO/MT-XVI, was notified on 1 July 2026 for vacancies of 2027-28, with online registration from 1 to 21 July 2026. The tentative schedule in the notification placed the prelims in August 2026, the mains in October 2026, interviews in November and December 2026, and provisional allotment in January 2027.

Candidates must be at least 20 and at most 30 years old on 1 July 2026, which means born between 2 July 1996 and 1 July 2006, both dates included. The notification lists category-wise relaxations of the upper age limit.

The educational requirement is a degree in any discipline from a university recognised by the Government of India, or an equivalent qualification. You must already be a graduate on the day you register, and the result must have been declared on or before 21 July 2026. The percentage in graduation has to be entered while registering, with CGPA converted as per the university's norms.

The preliminary examination is an objective test of three separately timed sections.

Note that the questions and marks are not one-to-one in the 2026 pattern: Reasoning carries 40 marks for 35 questions, while Quantitative Aptitude carries 30 for 35. You must clear the cut-off set by IBPS in each of the three tests, not just the total, to be shortlisted for the mains.

The main examination has four objective tests, which are not held in a fixed sequence, followed by a descriptive paper.

Together that is 190 minutes, the 3 hours 10 minutes the notification gives for the mains. The descriptive paper is in English and may be broadly based on economic and social issues, emerging trends in banking and technology, current events and ethics. IBPS says it may be marked by an automated scoring mechanism that looks for features of writing proficiency.

Each mains test and the overall total have their own minimum scores. Only those who clear them are called for the personality test and interview.

In every objective test of both the prelims and the mains, a wrong answer costs one fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to that question. A question left blank costs nothing. Because marks per question differ between tests, so does the size of the penalty.

Worked example: the mains Reasoning test

Reasoning carries 60 marks for 40 questions, so each question is worth 1.5 marks and a wrong answer costs 0.25 × 1.5 = 0.375.

A candidate with 30 correct and 6 wrong scores 30 × 1.5 − 6 × 0.375 = 45 − 2.25 = 42.75 out of 60.

Scores from different sessions are normalised using the equi-percentile method before cut-offs are applied.

Candidates who qualify in the mains take a self-report personality test. It is non-qualifying, but appearing in it is mandatory for the interview, and the personality profile is placed before the interview panel.

The common interview is conducted by the participating banks and co-ordinated by a nodal bank in each state or region. It carries 100 marks, with a minimum of 40% (35% for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD candidates). The final score combines the mains and the interview in the ratio 80:20. Prelims marks play no part in it.

Candidates high enough in the merit are then provisionally allotted to a bank, taking into account vacancies, reservation rules and their preferences.

Do prelims marks count in the final IBPS PO merit?

No. The notification says only the marks obtained in the online main examination are considered for shortlisting for the personality test and interview and for the final merit list. The prelims is a screening stage, and you must clear the cut-off in each of its three tests.

How is the final score worked out?

The main examination and the interview are combined in an 80:20 ratio. The interview is out of 100 and has a minimum qualifying mark of 40% (35% for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD candidates). You must qualify both the mains and the interview.

Why does each question not carry the same marks?

IBPS fixes the questions and marks for each test separately. In the 2026 mains, for example, 40 Reasoning questions carry 60 marks while 40 English questions carry 20, so a wrong answer costs a different amount in each test: one fourth of that question's own marks.
