Science & Tech Cheat Code: 20 Must-Know Facts (2024–25)

Published on December 24, 2025 | For UPSC, SSC & Banking Exams

Science & Technology is a **dynamic yet predictable** section in UPSC Prelims and SSC exams — and **8–10 questions come from recent developments**. Master these 20 Science & Tech facts (2024–2025) to secure **8+ free marks**.

1 Arka (11.77 petaflops, IITM Pune) and Arunika (8.24 petaflops, NCMRWF Noida) are HPC systems for weather and climate research, inaugurated by PM Modi in September 2024 under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

2 Agnibaan SOrTeD was launched by Agnikul Cosmos on May 30, 2024 from ‘Dhanush’, India’s first private launchpad, at Sriharikota. It was India’s second privately built rocket (after Skyroot’s Vikram-S, 2022).

3 GEMCOVAC-19 is India’s first indigenously developed mRNA vaccine, granted emergency use authorisation in June 2022 (by Gennova Biopharmaceuticals, Pune).

4 Micron Technology’s semiconductor ATMP (assembly & test) facility in Sanand, Gujarat was approved in June 2023 under the India Semiconductor Mission; commercial production was scheduled for early 2026.

5 The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified on November 13, 2025, operationalising the DPDP Act, 2023. Provisions for the Data Protection Board took effect immediately; most obligations apply after 18 months.

6 Chandrayaan-4 was announced by ISRO in November 2025 as a lunar sample-return mission targeting 2027 launch.

7 SSLV (Small Satellite Launch Vehicle) completed its developmental flights with SSLV-D3, which placed EOS-08 in orbit on August 16, 2024.

8 Namo Bharat (RRTS) trains, run by NCRTC, began on the Delhi–Meerut corridor’s Sahibabad–Duhai section on October 20, 2023.

9 SwasthVayu is an AI-integrated ventilator developed by CSIR, upgraded and relaunched in March 2024.

10 The IndiaAI Mission was approved by the Union Cabinet in March 2024 with an outlay of about ₹10,372 crore over five years.

11 India’s rank in Global Innovation Index 2025 is 38th of 139 (up from 39th in 2024 and 40th in 2023).

12 Bailadila (Chhattisgarh) is a major source of iron ore; Khetri (Rajasthan) for copper.

13 5G-enabled smart port was launched at JNPT (Nhava Sheva) in March 2024 using AI and IoT.

14 ISRO’s Aditya-L1 mission (India’s first solar observatory) successfully entered halo orbit around Lagrange Point 1 in January 2024.

15 Quantum Computing Lab was inaugurated by DRDO & IIT Madras in 2024 to advance India’s quantum capabilities.

16 UPI (Unified Payments Interface) crossed 11 billion transactions/month in November 2025.

17 PM-WANI (Wi-Fi Access Network Interface) aims to provide public Wi-Fi access through small entrepreneurs.

18 National Quantum Mission was approved in 2023 with a budget of ₹6,003 crore for 2023–2031.

19 Green Hydrogen Mission targets 5 MMT annual production by 2030 with an outlay of ₹19,744 crore.

20 India’s first indigenous gene therapy for Haemophilia A was developed by IIT Bombay & KEM Hospital in 2024.
