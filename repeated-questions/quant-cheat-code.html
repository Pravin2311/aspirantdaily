Quant Cheat Code: 20 Must-Know Shortcuts & Formulas

Published on December 25, 2025 | For SSC, Bank & Railway Exams

Quantitative Aptitude is the most scoring section in SSC and Banking exams — if you know the shortcuts. Master these 20 Quant cheat codes to solve questions in half the time.

1 Percentage Trick:
A% of B = B% of A
Example: 25% of 40 = 40% of 25 = 10

2 Time & Work:
If A = 10 days, B = 15 days → Together = (10×15)/(10+15) = 6 days

3 Speed, Time, Distance:
Always convert km/h to m/s: ×5/18
e.g., 72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s

4 Divisibility by 11:
(Sum of digits at odd places) – (Sum at even places) = 0 or multiple of 11
e.g., 121 → (1+1) – 2 = 0 → divisible by 11

5 Squares to Memorize:
1²=1, 2²=4, ..., 25²=625

6 Cubes to Memorize:
1³=1, 2³=8, ..., 15³=3375

7 Profit & Loss:
Profit % = (Profit/CP) × 100
Loss % = (Loss/CP) × 100

8 Simple Interest:
SI = (P × R × T)/100

9 Compound Interest:
A = P(1 + R/100)T

10 Average Speed (equal distance):
= (2 × S1 × S2) / (S1 + S2)

11 Pipes & Cisterns:
Same as Time & Work — filling = positive, emptying = negative

12 Ratio & Proportion:
If A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5 → A:B:C = 8:12:15 (make B same)

13 Algebra Identity:
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

14 Geometry: Triangle Area:
= ½ × base × height

15 Circle: Area & Circumference:
Area = πr², Circumference = 2πr

16 Data Interpretation Tip:
Always read the question first, then look at the graph/table.

17 DI: Percentage Calculation:
Part/Whole × 100 → round numbers for approximation

18 Number Series: Look for patterns:
+, –, ×, ÷, squares, cubes, alternate series

19 LCM & HCF:
Product of two numbers = LCM × HCF

20 Mixtures & Alligation:
(Cheaper quantity) : (Dearer quantity) = (d – m) : (m – c)

✍️ 6 Worked Examples Using These Codes

1. Successive percentage change. A price rises 20% and then falls 20%. Net change = a + b + ab/100 = 20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%. The price ends 4% lower, not unchanged.

2. Train crossing. A 200 m train at 72 km/h crosses a 300 m platform. Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s; distance = 200 + 300 = 500 m; time = 500 ÷ 20 = 25 seconds. To cross a pole, only the train's own length counts (10 s here).

3. Boats and streams. A boat goes 12 km/h downstream and 8 km/h upstream. Speed in still water = (12 + 8)/2 = 10 km/h; stream speed = (12 − 8)/2 = 2 km/h.

4. CI minus SI. For 2 years, CI − SI = P(R/100)². On ₹5,000 at 10%: 5,000 × 0.01 = ₹50.

5. Work by the LCM method. A takes 12 days and B 18 days. Take total work = LCM(12, 18) = 36 units, so A does 3 units/day and B 2. Together: 36 ÷ 5 = 7.2 days.

6. Number of factors. Write the number as prime powers and multiply (each power + 1). 72 = 2³ × 3², so it has (3+1)(2+1) = 12 factors.

Five More Codes

  • Sum of the first n natural numbers = n(n + 1)/2; sum of the first n odd numbers = n².
  • Squares of numbers ending in 5: 65² → 6 × 7 = 42, then append 25 → 4225.
  • Areas of similar triangles are in the ratio of the squares of their corresponding sides.
  • If x% of a quantity is taken away, the rest is (100 − x)% — useful in successive discount questions.
  • Average of consecutive numbers = (first + last)/2.
⚠️ Common traps: A 20% rise followed by a 20% fall is not zero change. Average speed for equal distances is the harmonic mean, not (S1 + S2)/2. In train problems, add the platform length to the train length.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fastest way to solve time and work questions?

Use the LCM method: take total work as the LCM of the given days, find each person's units per day, then divide. It avoids fractions and is quicker than the 1/x approach.

How do I calculate net change after two successive percentage changes?

Use a + b + ab/100, writing decreases as negative numbers. For +20% and −20%, the net change is −4%.

Which Quant topics should I learn first for SSC and bank exams?

Percentage, ratio, profit and loss, simple and compound interest, time and work, and speed–distance. Most other arithmetic questions build on these.
