Polity Cheat Code: 25 Must-Know MCQs (Repeated 2020–2025)

Last updated: September 19, 2026 | For UPSC Prelims & SSC Exams

These 25 Polity MCQs have appeared repeatedly in UPSC Prelims and SSC exams from 2020 to 2025. Master them once, and you’ll score 10+ free marks every year — no guesswork needed.

1 The ‘Basic Structure Doctrine’ was propounded in which case?

A. Golaknath Case
B. Kesavananda Bharati Case
C. Minerva Mills Case
D. S.R. Bommai Case
Answer: B. Kesavananda Bharati Case
Appeared in UPSC 2021, 2023, 2025 and SSC CGL 2022, 2024.

2 Which Article provides for the Finance Commission?

A. Article 260
B. Article 270
C. Article 280
D. Article 290
Answer: C. Article 280
Asked in UPSC 2020, 2022, 2024 and SSC CHSL 2023.

3 The ‘Right to Constitutional Remedies’ is under:

A. Article 32
B. Article 226
C. Article 323B
D. Article 356
Answer: A. Article 32
Dr. Ambedkar called it the “heart and soul” of the Constitution. Repeated every year.

4 Which Schedule deals with Rajya Sabha seat allocation?

A. Third Schedule
B. Fourth Schedule
C. Fifth Schedule
D. Sixth Schedule
Answer: B. Fourth Schedule
SSC loves this — appeared in CGL 2020, 2022, 2024.

5 ‘Doctrine of Pith and Substance’ is related to:

A. Validity of laws
B. Residuary powers
C. True nature of legislation
D. Fundamental Rights
Answer: C. True nature of legislation
Key for federalism questions in UPSC Prelims.

6 Fundamental Rights are contained in which Part of the Constitution?

A. Part II
B. Part III
C. Part IV
D. Part V
Answer: B. Part III
Part III (Articles 12–35) is often called the "Magna Carta" of the Indian Constitution — a near-certain Prelims topic every year.

7 The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSPs) are borrowed from the constitution of which country?

A. USA
B. UK
C. Ireland
D. Canada
Answer: C. Ireland
DPSPs (Part IV) were inspired by the Irish Free State Constitution of 1937 — a classic "which country inspired which feature" question.

8 Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by which Amendment?

A. 42nd Amendment, 1976
B. 44th Amendment, 1978
C. 52nd Amendment, 1985
D. 61st Amendment, 1988
Answer: A. 42nd Amendment, 1976
Added as Part IV-A (Article 51A) on the recommendation of the Swaran Singh Committee. The 42nd Amendment is also called the "Mini Constitution" for its sweeping changes.

9 Under which Article can the President promulgate Ordinances?

A. Article 110
B. Article 123
C. Article 155
D. Article 213
Answer: B. Article 123
Article 123 covers Presidential ordinances; Article 213 is the parallel Governor's power at the state level — the two are frequently mixed up in options.

10 Who appoints the Governor of a State?

A. Prime Minister
B. Chief Minister
C. President of India
D. Chief Justice of India
Answer: C. President of India
Under Article 155, the Governor is appointed by the President and holds office at the President's pleasure (Article 156).

11 What is the minimum age required to become President of India?

A. 25 years
B. 30 years
C. 35 years
D. 40 years
Answer: C. 35 years
Article 58. A common trap: 25 years is the minimum for the Lok Sabha, 30 for the Rajya Sabha — exams love testing all three together.

12 A Money Bill is defined under which Article of the Constitution?

A. Article 108
B. Article 110
C. Article 112
D. Article 114
Answer: B. Article 110
A Money Bill can only be introduced in the Lok Sabha and the Rajya Sabha cannot amend it — high-frequency in both Polity and current-affairs-linked questions.

13 What is the term of office of the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) of India?

A. 5 years or 62 years of age, whichever is earlier
B. 6 years or 65 years of age, whichever is earlier
C. 6 years, fixed
D. Till the pleasure of the President
Answer: B. 6 years or 65 years of age, whichever is earlier
Article 148. The CAG is described by Dr. Ambedkar as one of the most important officers under the Constitution — a recurring Prelims favorite.

14 The Right to Property was removed from the list of Fundamental Rights by which Amendment?

A. 42nd Amendment, 1976
B. 44th Amendment, 1978
C. 73rd Amendment, 1992
D. 86th Amendment, 2002
Answer: B. 44th Amendment, 1978
Property was downgraded from a Fundamental Right to a mere legal right under the new Article 300-A — a favorite "which right was removed" question.

15 The concept of ‘Judicial Review’ in the Indian Constitution is inspired by the constitution of which country?

A. UK
B. USA
C. Canada
D. Australia
Answer: B. USA
India borrowed Judicial Review from the US Constitution, while Parliamentary government was borrowed from the UK — a classic pairing question.

16 Article 356 of the Constitution deals with:

A. National Emergency
B. Financial Emergency
C. President's Rule in a state
D. Suspension of Fundamental Rights
Answer: C. President's Rule in a state
Invoked on failure of constitutional machinery in a state; famously scrutinized in the S.R. Bommai case (1994), which limited its misuse.

17 Which Amendment lowered the voting age in India from 21 to 18 years?

A. 52nd Amendment, 1985
B. 61st Amendment, 1988
C. 73rd Amendment, 1992
D. 91st Amendment, 2003
Answer: B. 61st Amendment, 1988
Amended Article 326. A straightforward but consistently repeated static-GK-style Polity question.

18 The Election Commission of India is established under which Article?

A. Article 315
B. Article 324
C. Article 335
D. Article 343
Answer: B. Article 324
Article 324 gives the ECI superintendence, direction, and control over elections — frequently paired with questions on the CEC's tenure and removal process.

19 Who was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly?

A. Jawaharlal Nehru
B. Rajendra Prasad
C. B.R. Ambedkar
D. Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
Answer: C. B.R. Ambedkar
Dr. Rajendra Prasad chaired the Constituent Assembly itself, while Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired its Drafting Committee — a common mix-up exams exploit.

20 The Ninth Schedule of the Constitution is significant because it:

A. Lists the official languages of India
B. Protects certain laws from judicial review on Fundamental Rights grounds
C. Deals with anti-defection
D. Allocates Rajya Sabha seats to states
Answer: B. Protects certain laws from judicial review on Fundamental Rights grounds
Added by the 1st Amendment (1951); the I.R. Coelho case (2007) later held that Ninth Schedule laws are still subject to Basic Structure review.

21 Article 44 of the Constitution, as a Directive Principle, calls for:

A. Free legal aid
B. A Uniform Civil Code
C. Separation of judiciary from executive
D. Promotion of cottage industries
Answer: B. A Uniform Civil Code
A recurring current-affairs-linked static question given the ongoing UCC debate (see our Current Affairs notes on the topic).

22 The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 relates to:

A. Panchayati Raj Institutions
B. Municipalities
C. Anti-defection law
D. Goods and Services Tax
Answer: A. Panchayati Raj Institutions
Gave constitutional status to rural local self-government (added Part IX). Its urban counterpart, the 74th Amendment, covers Municipalities — always tested as a pair.

23 The 74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 relates to:

A. Panchayati Raj Institutions
B. Municipalities
C. Cooperative societies
D. Tribal Areas
Answer: B. Municipalities
Added Part IX-A, giving constitutional status to urban local bodies — the direct urban counterpart of the 73rd Amendment above.

24 India's anti-defection law was introduced through which Schedule?

A. Eighth Schedule
B. Ninth Schedule
C. Tenth Schedule
D. Eleventh Schedule
Answer: C. Tenth Schedule
Added by the 52nd Amendment, 1985. A near-guaranteed question whenever defection cases are in the news — pair it with the Kihoto Hollohan case (1992).

25 Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Right under the Indian Constitution?

A. Right to Equality
B. Right against Exploitation
C. Right to Property
D. Right to Constitutional Remedies
Answer: C. Right to Property
Ties directly back to Q14 above — since the 44th Amendment (1978), Right to Property is only a legal right under Article 300-A, not a Fundamental Right.
