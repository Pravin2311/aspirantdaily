❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Who won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2025?

María Corina Machado of Venezuela won the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize for her work promoting democratic rights in Venezuela.

Which is the highest peak in India — K2 or Kangchenjunga?

By India's official position K2 (8,611 m) is the highest, since it lies in Pakistan-occupied territory that India claims. Kangchenjunga (8,586 m) is the highest peak within Indian-administered territory. Choose based on how the question is worded.

Who was the first Indian to go to the International Space Station?

Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla, on the Axiom-4 mission in June–July 2025. Rakesh Sharma (1984) was the first Indian in space.
