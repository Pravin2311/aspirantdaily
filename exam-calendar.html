How to read an exam calendar

  • A calendar is not a notification. Annual or tentative calendars give planned dates. The detailed notification (eligibility, vacancies, fees, application window) is a separate document, and its dates take precedence.
  • Tentative dates move. Commissions often reschedule exams after a calendar is published. Check the "Latest News" or "Notices" section of the official website closer to the date.
  • Exam dates and shift details are announced separately. City intimation slips and admit cards, released shortly before the exam, carry your actual date, shift and centre.
  • Beware of unofficial sites. Coaching and news sites (including this one) can carry errors. Treat the PDF notice on the official domain as the only authority.
