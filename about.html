About AspirantDaily

Free practice and study notes for Indian competitive exams

What we publish

AspirantDaily is a free website for students preparing for SSC, Banking, Railway (RRB), UPSC and State PSC exams. Nothing on the site needs a login. It has:

  • Daily practice sets of multiple-choice questions by subject, on the home page.
  • Study notes in the Study Portal: polity, history, geography, economy, environment, science and technology, international relations, government schemes, current affairs, and quantitative aptitude and reasoning methods with worked examples.
  • Guides on the blog: syllabus and exam-pattern pages, exam comparisons and preparation methods.
  • Revision sheets in Repeated Questions, and an exam calendar built from official notices.

Where our information comes from

We rely on official, primary sources wherever they exist:

  • Exam rules, dates and syllabi: the notices of the conducting body, such as UPSC, SSC, the RRBs, IBPS, SBI and the State Public Service Commissions.
  • Laws and the Constitution: the text published by the Legislative Department (legislative.gov.in) and the Gazette of India.
  • Government schemes and current affairs: Press Information Bureau (PIB) releases and ministry websites.
  • Banking and the economy: the Reserve Bank of India, the Union Budget documents and official statistics.
  • School-level concepts: NCERT textbooks.

Where a fact depends on a news report rather than an official document, we say so or leave it out. Exam-calendar entries link to the notice they come from.

How pages are checked

  • Pages show when they were last updated. When a rule, figure or scheme changes, we update the page and its date.
  • In October 2026 we re-checked every study note, guide and revision sheet against current official sources. We corrected outdated facts and removed figures we could not verify, such as unsourced cut-offs, salaries and "question analysis" claims.
  • Newer method and topic pages pass automated checks before publishing. Every worked-example calculation is recomputed, the text is compared against existing pages to avoid duplication, and the sources are listed. A person on our team then reviews and approves each page.
  • We do not claim that a topic is "asked every year" or that a page is based on previous-year papers unless we have actually checked those papers.

What we are not

  • AspirantDaily is not affiliated with UPSC, SSC, IBPS, SBI, the RRBs, any State PSC or any government body. Always confirm dates, eligibility and syllabus in the official notice before you apply.
  • We do not promise results or selection. Our pages are study aids.

Advertising

The site is free because it shows ads through Google AdSense. Advertisers have no say in what we publish, and ads are not placed inside the questions or notes. See our Privacy Policy for how ads and analytics use cookies.

Found an error?

Email support@teachings.ai with the page link and what looks wrong. If you can, include the official source. We check each report, correct the page if needed and update its date. For a quiz question, include the subject and question number.
