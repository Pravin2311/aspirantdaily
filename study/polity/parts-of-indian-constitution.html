Parts of the Indian Constitution: All 25 Parts with Their Headings and Article Ranges

Part I to XXII • IVA, IXA, IXB and XIVA • Part VII omitted • Article ranges • Which Amendment added which Part • Memory aids

UPSC SSC State PSC Railway 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-04

The Constitution's Articles are grouped into Parts, numbered I to XXII, with lettered Parts (IVA, IXA, IXB, XIVA) added later by amendments. Part VII has been omitted, so 25 Parts are in force today. This table follows the headings in the official text published by the Legislative Department, as on 10 September 2026.

Some Article numbers within these ranges have been omitted or are no longer in force (Article 238 went with Part VII, and Article 31 with the right to property). The ranges show where each Part sits, not how many Articles are active.

Four lettered Parts were inserted after 1950. Exams often ask which Amendment added which.

PartSubjectInserted by
IVAFundamental Duties (Article 51A)42nd Amendment (1976)
IXAThe Municipalities74th Amendment (1992)
IXBThe Co-operative Societies97th Amendment (2011)
XIVATribunals42nd Amendment (1976)

Part IX, The Panchayats, is not a lettered Part, but it is also an addition. The original Part IX was omitted by the 7th Amendment (1956), and the present Part IX was inserted by the 73rd Amendment (1992). Part VII, which dealt with the Part B States of the 1950 First Schedule, was omitted by the 7th Amendment (1956) when the old A, B, C and D classification of States was abolished.

  • Rights block, Parts III, IV and IVA: rights (12 to 35), directives (36 to 51), duties (51A).
  • Government block, Parts V and VI: the Union (52 to 151) mirrors the States (152 to 237). Each has an executive chapter, a legislature chapter and a judiciary chapter.
  • Local government, Parts IX, IXA and IXB: panchayats, municipalities and co-operatives, all in the 243 series.
  • "One Article" Parts: IVA (51A) and XX (368).
  • The last four: XIX miscellaneous, XX amendment, XXI special provisions (including Article 370 and 371 to 371J), XXII commencement and repeals.

The Schedules sit outside the Parts and are listed on the Schedules of the Constitution. The most-asked individual Articles are on important Articles, and the State-specific provisions in Part XXI are on Article 371 special provisions.

Q1. Part IXB of the Constitution deals with: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Co-operative Societies

💡 Explanation: Part IXB, The Co-operative Societies, was inserted by the 97th Amendment (2011). IXA is Municipalities and XIVA is Tribunals.

Q2. Which Part of the Constitution has been omitted? easy

✅ Answer: (a) Part VII

💡 Explanation: Part VII, The States in Part B of the First Schedule, was omitted when the A/B/C/D classification of States ended in 1956.

Q3. Emergency Provisions are in Part: easy

✅ Answer: (d) XVIII

💡 Explanation: Part XVIII, Articles 352 to 360. XVI is special provisions for certain classes, XVII official language and XIX miscellaneous.

Q4. Which two lettered Parts were inserted by the 42nd Amendment? medium

✅ Answer: (b) IVA and XIVA

💡 Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Part IVA (Fundamental Duties) and Part XIVA (Tribunals). IXA came with the 74th and IXB with the 97th.

Q5. Part XIII deals with: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Trade, Commerce and Intercourse within India

💡 Explanation: Part XIII, Articles 301 to 307. XII is finance, XIV services and XV elections.

How many Parts does the Indian Constitution have?

The Parts are numbered I to XXII, plus four lettered Parts (IVA, IXA, IXB, XIVA), which makes 26. Part VII has been omitted, so 25 are in force.

Which Part has only one Article?

Part IVA (Article 51A, Fundamental Duties) and Part XX (Article 368, Amendment of the Constitution).
