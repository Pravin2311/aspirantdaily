Important Amendments of the Indian Constitution: 1st to 106th, With Year, Date in Force and What Each Changed

1st: Ninth Schedule • 7th: States reorganised • 24th • 36th: Sikkim • 42nd: 'socialist secular', duties • 44th: property right removed • 52nd: anti-defection • 61st: voting age 18 • 73rd/74th • 86th • 101st: GST • 103rd: EWS • 106th: women's reservation

UPSC SSC Railway State PSC 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-04

Parliament amends the Constitution under Article 368, and each change is made by a numbered Constitution (Amendment) Act. The official text records every change in a footnote, such as "Ins. by the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976, s. 11 (w.e.f. 3-1-1977)". Here "Ins." means inserted, "Subs." means substituted, and "w.e.f." is the date the change took effect.

The year of an amendment Act and the date it came into force can differ. The 86th Amendment of 2002, for example, took effect only on 1 April 2010. Exams ask about both, so the table gives both where the official text records them.

  • 42nd (1976), the "mini-Constitution": the widest single amendment. It changed the Preamble's description of India and added Part IVA, the Fundamental Duties, in force from 3 January 1977. See Fundamental Duties, Article 51A.
  • 44th (1978): undid parts of the 42nd. Its best-known change omitted Article 31, so property is now protected only by Article 300A, outside the Fundamental Rights.
  • 52nd (1985): the anti-defection law in the Tenth Schedule. See anti-defection law.
  • 73rd and 74th (1992): local self-government. See the 73rd Amendment.
  • 101st (2016): the Goods and Services Tax, with Article 246A giving both Parliament and the States power to tax supplies.
  • 106th (2023): one-third reservation for women. See the 106th Amendment.

Group the amendments by theme rather than by number. Rights: 1st, 24th, 44th, 86th. Federal structure and States: 7th, 36th, 101st, 105th. Elections and Parliament: 52nd, 61st, 91st, 104th, 106th. Local bodies and co-operatives: 73rd, 74th, 97th. Social justice: 102nd, 103rd, 104th.

Watch the gap between the year of the Act and its date in force: the 44th (1978 Act, mostly in force 1979), the 61st (1988 Act, in force 1989), the 73rd and 74th (1992 Acts, in force 1993) and the 86th (2002 Act, in force 2010). For the list of Articles these amendments touch, see important articles of the Constitution.

Q1. Which amendment lowered the voting age from 21 to 18 years? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 61st

💡 Explanation: The 61st Amendment, 1988, replaced 'twenty-one years' in Article 326; it came into force on 28 March 1989.

Q2. The Tenth Schedule (anti-defection) was added by the: easy

✅ Answer: (a) 52nd Amendment, 1985

💡 Explanation: The official text records the Tenth Schedule as added by the 52nd Amendment, 1985, in force 1 March 1985. The 91st Amendment later changed it.

Q3. Which amendment removed the right to property (Article 31) from the Fundamental Rights? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 44th

💡 Explanation: The 44th Amendment, 1978, omitted Article 31 with effect from 20 June 1979; Article 300A now protects property as a constitutional right.

Q4. Sikkim became a State of India through which amendment? medium

✅ Answer: (a) 36th

💡 Explanation: The 36th Amendment, 1975, inserted Article 371F, with special provisions for Sikkim, in force 26 April 1975.

Q5. Which of these amendments was struck down by the Supreme Court? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 99th (NJAC)

💡 Explanation: The official text notes that the 99th Amendment, 2014, which created the National Judicial Appointments Commission, has been struck down by the Supreme Court.

Why is the 42nd Amendment called the 'mini-Constitution'?

Because it changed so much at once in 1976: the Preamble's description of India, a new Part IVA of Fundamental Duties, new Directive Principles and many other provisions. Most of it took effect on 3 January 1977.

Which amendments created panchayats and municipalities?

The 73rd Amendment, 1992, inserted Part IX (Panchayats), in force 24 April 1993. The 74th Amendment, 1992, inserted Part IXA (Municipalities), in force 1 June 1993.

How many times has the Constitution been amended?

The latest amendment covered on this page is the 106th, of 2023, on women's reservation. Check the Legislative Department's list for any newer amendment Acts.
