DPSP and Fundamental Duties MCQs: 25 Questions on Articles 36 to 51A With Article-Wise Answers

Article 37 • 38 and 39 • 39A, 43A, 48A (42nd Amendment) • 43B (97th) • 45 (86th) • 44 UCC • 51A duties • 51A(k)

UPSC SSC State PSC 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-06

These 25 original questions cover Part IV (Directive Principles of State Policy, Articles 36 to 51) and Part IVA (Fundamental Duties, Article 51A). Twenty test the Directive Principles and five test the Fundamental Duties. Each answer names the Article it comes from.

Most questions on this topic do one of two things. They match a principle to its Article number, or they ask which amendment added a principle. Learn the amendment table below before you start.

  • Not enforceable, but fundamental. Article 37 says the Directive Principles "shall not be enforceable by any court", but they are "fundamental in the governance of the country", and the State has a duty to apply them in making laws.
  • Duties are for citizens. Article 51A begins "It shall be the duty of every citizen of India". The Directive Principles, by contrast, are addressed to "the State", with the same meaning as in Part III.
  • Article 45 changed meaning. After the 86th Amendment, Article 45 is about children below six. Education from six to fourteen became a Fundamental Right under Article 21A and, for parents, a Fundamental Duty under 51A(k).

Q1. Directive Principles are described in Article 37 as: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Not enforceable by any court but fundamental in governance

💡 Explanation: Article 37: not enforceable by any court, yet fundamental in governance, and the State must apply them in making laws.

Q2. Minimising inequalities in income is a directive under: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Article 38(2)

💡 Explanation: Article 38(2), inserted by the 44th Amendment (1978), asks the State to minimise income inequalities.

Q3. Distribution of ownership and control of material resources to subserve the common good is in: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Article 39(b)

💡 Explanation: Article 39(b). Article 39(c) is about preventing concentration of wealth.

Q4. Equal pay for equal work for both men and women is a directive under: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Article 39(d)

💡 Explanation: Article 39(d) is a Directive Principle; Articles 14 and 16 are Fundamental Rights.

Q5. Equal justice and free legal aid were added as Article 39A by the: easy

✅ Answer: (a) 42nd Amendment

💡 Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Article 39A, in force from 3 January 1977.

Q6. Organisation of village panchayats is a directive under: easy

✅ Answer: (b) Article 40

💡 Explanation: Article 40. Part IX, from Article 243, gives panchayats their constitutional structure.

Q7. The right to work, to education and to public assistance in cases of unemployment and old age is in: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Article 41

💡 Explanation: Article 41, within the limits of the State's economic capacity and development.

Q8. Just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief are provided for in: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Article 42

💡 Explanation: Article 42 covers just and humane conditions of work and maternity relief.

Q9. Promotion of cottage industries in rural areas is mentioned in: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Article 43

💡 Explanation: Article 43, on a living wage, also asks the State to promote cottage industries on an individual or co-operative basis.

Q10. Participation of workers in management of industries (Article 43A) was added by the: medium

✅ Answer: (b) 42nd Amendment

💡 Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Article 43A on workers' participation in management.

Q11. Promotion of co-operative societies (Article 43B) was added by the: medium

✅ Answer: (c) 97th Amendment

💡 Explanation: The 97th Amendment (2011) inserted Article 43B, in force from 15 February 2012.

Q12. The Uniform Civil Code is a directive under: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Article 44

💡 Explanation: Article 44: the State shall endeavour to secure a uniform civil code throughout India.

Q13. After the 86th Amendment, Article 45 deals with: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Early childhood care and education until age six

💡 Explanation: Article 45 now covers children until they complete six. Ages six to fourteen moved to Article 21A.

Q14. Promoting educational and economic interests of SCs, STs and weaker sections is in: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Article 46

💡 Explanation: Article 46 is the Directive Principle; Articles 15(4) and 16(4) are enabling Fundamental Rights provisions.

Q15. Prohibition of intoxicating drinks, except for medicinal purposes, is a directive under: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Article 47

💡 Explanation: Article 47, along with raising nutrition, standard of living and public health.

Q16. Prohibiting slaughter of cows, calves and other milch and draught cattle is mentioned in: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Article 48

💡 Explanation: Article 48, on organising agriculture and animal husbandry on modern and scientific lines.

Q17. Protection of the environment, forests and wildlife as a Directive Principle is in: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Article 48A

💡 Explanation: Article 48A, inserted by the 42nd Amendment. Article 51A(g) is the matching citizens' duty.

Q18. Protection of monuments of national importance is a directive under: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Article 49

💡 Explanation: Article 49 covers monuments declared by or under Parliament's law to be of national importance.

Q19. Separation of the judiciary from the executive is a directive under: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Article 50

💡 Explanation: Article 50 asks the State to separate the judiciary from the executive in public services.

Q20. Encouraging settlement of international disputes by arbitration appears in: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Article 51

💡 Explanation: Article 51(d), among the directives on international peace and security.

Q21. Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by the: easy

✅ Answer: (a) 42nd Amendment

💡 Explanation: The 42nd Amendment (1976) inserted Part IVA and Article 51A, in force from 3 January 1977.

Q22. The duty of a parent or guardian to provide education to a child aged six to fourteen is in: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Article 51A(k)

💡 Explanation: Article 51A(k), added by the 86th Amendment. Article 21A is the matching Fundamental Right.

Q23. Developing the scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry is a Fundamental Duty under: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Article 51A(h)

💡 Explanation: Article 51A(h) lists scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry and reform.

Q24. Valuing and preserving the rich heritage of our composite culture is the duty in: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Article 51A(f)

💡 Explanation: Article 51A(f) asks every citizen to value and preserve our composite culture.

Q25. Fundamental Duties under Article 51A are addressed to: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Every citizen of India

💡 Explanation: Article 51A opens: 'It shall be the duty of every citizen of India'.

How many Fundamental Duties are there?

Article 51A lists eleven duties, (a) to (k). The first ten came with the 42nd Amendment and clause (k) was added by the 86th Amendment.

Which Directive Principles were added by the 42nd Amendment?

Articles 39A (free legal aid), 43A (workers' participation) and 48A (environment) were inserted, and clause (f) of Article 39 was substituted.
