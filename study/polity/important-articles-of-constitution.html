This page lists 101 important articles of the Indian Constitution, grouped by Part, with each Article's official heading as printed in the Constitution of India (Legislative Department edition as on 10 September 2026). Headings are copied from the text, so they are the exact words an exam may quote. The third column adds a short memory aid where one helps.

The Constitution runs from Part I to Part XXII, with lettered Parts such as IVA and IXA added later, and has 12 Schedules. Exams test a small core again and again: the Fundamental Rights, the President and Parliament, the courts, the Governor, emergencies and amendment. Learn the Article number and its heading together, then use the linked topic pages for detail.

Q1. Which Article deals with the 'Abolition of Untouchability'? easy

✅ Answer: (c) Article 17

💡 Explanation: Article 17 is headed 'Abolition of Untouchability'; Article 18 abolishes titles.

Q2. The 'Uniform civil code for the citizens' is found in: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Article 44

💡 Explanation: Article 44 in Part IV (Directive Principles) asks the State to endeavour to secure a uniform civil code.

Q3. Which Article lets the Supreme Court grant special leave to appeal? medium

✅ Answer: (b) Article 136

💡 Explanation: Article 136 is 'Special leave to appeal by the Supreme Court'; 137 covers review and 141 makes its law binding.

Q4. Under which Article does the Governor deal with Bills passed by the State Legislature? medium

✅ Answer: (d) Article 200

💡 Explanation: Article 200 is 'Assent to Bills' for State legislatures; Article 111 is the President's counterpart and 213 covers the Governor's ordinances.

Q5. 'Adjudication of disputes relating to waters of inter-State rivers or river valleys' is the heading of: hard

✅ Answer: (a) Article 262

💡 Explanation: Article 262 deals with inter-State river disputes; 263 provides for an inter-State Council.

Which Article is called the right to constitutional remedies?

Article 32, headed 'Remedies for enforcement of rights conferred by this Part'. It guarantees the right to move the Supreme Court to enforce fundamental rights. Article 226 gives High Courts their own writ power.

Which Articles cover the three emergencies?

Article 352 (Proclamation of Emergency), Article 356 (failure of constitutional machinery in States, popularly called President's rule) and Article 360 (financial emergency).

Is the right to property still a fundamental right?

No. It now sits in Article 300A, 'Persons not to be deprived of property save by authority of law', in Part XII, so it is a constitutional right rather than a fundamental right.
