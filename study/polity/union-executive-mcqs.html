Union Executive MCQs: 25 Questions on the President, Vice-President, Prime Minister and Council of Ministers

Articles 52 to 62 President • 61 impeachment • 63 to 71 Vice-President • 72 pardons • 74 and 75 Council of Ministers • 78 PM's duties

UPSC SSC State PSC 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-06

These 25 original questions cover Chapter I of Part V, the Union executive: the President (Articles 52 to 62), the Vice-President (63 to 71), the pardoning power (72), the Council of Ministers (74 and 75) and the Prime Minister's duties (78). Each answer names the Article it comes from.

Q1. The executive power of the Union is vested in: easy

✅ Answer: (a) The President

💡 Explanation: Article 53(1) vests it in the President, exercised directly or through subordinate officers.

Q2. Who is NOT part of the electoral college for the President? medium

✅ Answer: (b) Nominated Rajya Sabha members

💡 Explanation: Article 54 includes only elected members. Its Explanation includes Delhi and Puducherry Assemblies.

Q3. The minimum age to be elected President is: easy

✅ Answer: (c) 35

💡 Explanation: Article 58(1)(b): a candidate for President must have completed thirty-five years of age.

Q4. The President's term of office is: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Five years

💡 Explanation: Article 56(1): five years from the date of entering upon office.

Q5. Re-election of a person as President is: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Allowed, subject to other provisions

💡 Explanation: Article 57 makes a current or former President eligible for re-election, with no limit stated.

Q6. If an MP is elected President, their seat: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Is deemed vacated on entering office as President

💡 Explanation: Article 59(1): the seat is deemed vacated on the date the person enters upon office as President.

Q7. The President's oath is made in the presence of: easy

✅ Answer: (c) The Chief Justice of India

💡 Explanation: Article 60: before the Chief Justice of India or, in their absence, the senior-most Supreme Court Judge available.

Q8. The only ground for impeaching the President is: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Violation of the Constitution

💡 Explanation: Article 61(1): the President is impeached only for violation of the Constitution.

Q9. A resolution to impeach the President needs notice signed by at least: medium

✅ Answer: (a) One-fourth of the House

💡 Explanation: Article 61(2)(a): fourteen days' notice signed by not less than one-fourth of the total members.

Q10. The majority needed to prefer and to sustain an impeachment charge is: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Two-thirds of total membership of the House

💡 Explanation: Article 61(2)(b) and (4): two-thirds of the total membership, in each House at its stage.

Q11. An election to fill a vacancy caused by the President's death must be held within: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Six months

💡 Explanation: Article 62(2): as soon as possible and in no case later than six months.

Q12. A President elected to fill a mid-term vacancy serves: medium

✅ Answer: (d) A full five-year term

💡 Explanation: Article 62(2): a full term of five years from entering upon office.

Q13. The Vice-President is elected by: medium

✅ Answer: (a) All members of both Houses of Parliament

💡 Explanation: Article 66(1): an electoral college of the members of both Houses, nominated members included.

Q14. The Vice-President is ex officio Chairman of: easy

✅ Answer: (b) The Council of States

💡 Explanation: Article 64 makes the Vice-President ex officio Chairman of the Council of States.

Q15. If the President's office falls vacant, the Vice-President acts as President until: medium

✅ Answer: (c) A new President enters upon office

💡 Explanation: Article 65(1): until the new President, elected to fill the vacancy, enters upon office.

Q16. The Vice-President's oath is made before: hard

✅ Answer: (d) The President or a person appointed by the President

💡 Explanation: Article 69: before the President or some person appointed in that behalf.

Q17. Disputes about the election of the President are decided by: easy

✅ Answer: (a) The Supreme Court

💡 Explanation: Article 71(1): such disputes are decided by the Supreme Court, whose decision is final.

Q18. The President can pardon in cases of sentence by: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Court Martial, Union-law offences and death sentences

💡 Explanation: Article 72(1) covers Court Martial sentences, offences against Union laws and death sentences.

Q19. The Council of Ministers is headed by: easy

✅ Answer: (c) The Prime Minister

💡 Explanation: Article 74(1): a Council of Ministers with the Prime Minister at the head.

Q20. After reconsideration of its advice, the President: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Shall act in accordance with it

💡 Explanation: The proviso to Article 74(1), added by the 44th Amendment, makes reconsidered advice binding.

Q21. Whether Ministers tendered advice, and what advice, can be inquired into by: medium

✅ Answer: (a) No court

💡 Explanation: Article 74(2): it shall not be inquired into in any court.

Q22. Other Ministers are appointed by the President on the advice of: easy

✅ Answer: (b) The Prime Minister

💡 Explanation: Article 75(1): the President appoints other Ministers on the Prime Minister's advice.

Q23. The total number of Ministers, including the PM, cannot exceed: easy

✅ Answer: (c) 15% of the Lok Sabha

💡 Explanation: Article 75(1A), added by the 91st Amendment: fifteen per cent of the Lok Sabha's total membership.

Q24. The Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to: easy

✅ Answer: (d) The House of the People

💡 Explanation: Article 75(3): the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the House of the People.

Q25. The duty to communicate Cabinet decisions to the President rests on the Prime Minister under: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Article 78

💡 Explanation: Article 78(a) requires the PM to communicate decisions on administration and proposals for legislation.

Can a person be President more than twice?

Article 57 makes any present or former President eligible for re-election, subject to other provisions, and states no limit on the number of terms.

Who elects the Vice-President?

Under Article 66(1), all members of both Houses of Parliament, including nominated members, form the electoral college for the Vice-President.
