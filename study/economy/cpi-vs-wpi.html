CPI vs WPI: How Inflation Is Measured in India, With the New 2024 and 2022-23 Series

CPI by NSO-MoSPI, base 2024 • WPI by DPIIT, base 2022-23 • Retail vs bulk prices • Services in CPI only • Food weights • September 2026 data

UPSC SSC Banking 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-06

The Consumer Price Index (CPI) measures retail prices paid by households. The National Statistics Office (NSO) of the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) publishes it. The Wholesale Price Index (WPI) measures prices of goods at the first stage of bulk sale. The Office of the Economic Adviser in the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) publishes it. RBI's inflation target is set on CPI, not WPI.

Both indices got new base years in 2026. CPI now uses base 2024=100, and WPI uses base 2022-23.

In September 2026 WPI inflation (11.39%) was about twice CPI inflation (5.54%). DPIIT's FAQ gives four reasons the two indices can diverge:

  1. Stage of price: WPI uses first-stage bulk prices; CPI uses retail prices.
  2. Weights: WPI weights come from gross value of output; CPI weights come from household spending.
  3. Food and fuel weights: food is 34.77% of CPI but 24.99% of WPI, and weights of petroleum products such as petrol and diesel also differ widely.
  4. Basket: CPI includes services, which WPI does not. Much of WPI is manufacturing inputs and intermediate goods such as minerals, basic metals and machinery.

In September 2026 the gap was driven largely by fuel and power, which rose 25.85% on WPI. Mineral oils and crude petroleum were among the drivers DPIIT named.

  • The base moved from 2011-12 to 2022-23. It is the eighth revision, after 1952-53, 1961-62, 1970-71, 1981-82, 1993-94, 2004-05 and 2011-12.
  • The new series was first released on 15 June 2026, for May 2026.
  • A Working Group under Prof. Ramesh Chand recommended it. It was set up on 30 December 2024 and reported in April 2026.
  • Items rose from 697 to 957, and price quotations from 8,331 to 15,254.
  • Weights now use gross value of output instead of net traded value (GVO plus imports minus exports).
  • Solar, wind and nuclear electricity were added. Crude petroleum and natural gas moved from primary articles to fuel and power.
  • DPIIT also releases producer price indices alongside WPI: an Output PPI, a trial Input PPI for manufacturing, and Service PPIs for seven services.

WPI's base year normally follows the national accounts base year, because WPI is mainly used as a deflator for GDP at constant prices.

DPIIT's FAQ lists four main uses of WPI:

  • It gives an early estimate of inflation at the bulk transaction level, so the government can act on essential commodities before price increases spill over to retail prices.
  • It is the deflator for many sectors when estimating GDP, and it deflates nominal production values in the Index of Industrial Production (IIP).
  • Businesses use it for indexation in contracts.
  • Global investors track it as a key macro indicator.

CPI, by contrast, measures the cost of living faced by households, which is why it is the yardstick for monetary policy.

WPI vs producer price index (PPI)

An Output PPI measures prices received by producers at basic price, which excludes tax and trade and transport margins. An Input PPI measures prices producers pay for inputs, at purchaser's price. Since the 2004-05 to 2011-12 revision, WPI has also used basic price, so it is now close to the Output PPI. It still differs in three ways: it follows a traditional classification of goods, it does not collect prices from export-oriented units, and it leaves out services.

  • CPI: NSO (MoSPI), base 2024=100, includes services, used for the 4% inflation target.
  • WPI: Office of the Economic Adviser (DPIIT), base 2022-23, goods only, 957 items.
  • WPI major-group weights: primary 22.76%, fuel and power 14.11%, manufactured 63.13%.
  • September 2026: CPI 5.54%, WPI 11.39%.

What is the main difference between CPI and WPI?

CPI tracks retail prices paid by consumers and includes services. WPI tracks first-stage bulk prices of goods only, and its weights are based on the value of production.

Which index does RBI use for its inflation target?

RBI's target is 4% inflation measured on the Consumer Price Index, with a tolerance band of 2% to 6%. WPI is used mainly as a deflator for GDP and IIP.

Why was WPI inflation so much higher than CPI in September 2026?

Fuel and power prices rose 25.85% on WPI. Fuel and intermediate goods weigh more in WPI, while CPI includes services and has a different food weight.
