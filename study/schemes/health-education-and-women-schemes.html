  • Many were launched at specific places that examiners like to ask: Panipat (BBBP), Ballia (Ujjwala), Ranchi (PM-JAY), Jhunjhunu (POSHAN Abhiyaan).

Ayushman Bharat — Two Pillars

PillarWhat it does
Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) — launched September 23, 2018, RanchiHealth cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospital care, cashless at empanelled public and private hospitals; implemented by the National Health Authority
Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (formerly Health and Wellness Centres)Upgraded primary health centres offering comprehensive primary care, including screening for non-communicable diseases

Since October 2024, all citizens aged 70 and above are covered regardless of income through the Ayushman Vay Vandana card, with up to ₹5 lakh a year in cover. Two related schemes: Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (2021) gives every citizen a digital health ID (ABHA), and PM Jan Aushadhi sells quality generic medicines at low prices through Jan Aushadhi Kendras.

Immunisation

Mission Indradhanush (December 25, 2014) aims at full immunisation of children under two and pregnant women against vaccine-preventable diseases. India was certified polio-free by the WHO on March 27, 2014.

Nutrition

  • POSHAN Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission) — launched on March 8, 2018 at Jhunjhunu, Rajasthan, to reduce stunting, under-nutrition, anaemia and low birth weight. Now part of Saksham Anganwadi and POSHAN 2.0. September is observed as Poshan Maah.
  • PM POSHAN (2021) — the renamed Mid-Day Meal scheme, giving hot cooked meals to children in government and government-aided schools; run by the Ministry of Education.
  • PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana — free foodgrains to about 81 crore beneficiaries under the National Food Security Act; extended for five years from January 1, 2024.

Education

  • Samagra Shiksha (2018) — merged three older programmes (Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan and Teacher Education) into one scheme from pre-school to Class 12.
  • National Education Policy (July 29, 2020) — replaced the 1986 policy; introduced the 5+3+3+4 school structure.
  • PM SHRI (2022) — upgrades over 14,500 existing schools into model schools showcasing NEP 2020.
  • NIPUN Bharat (2021) — foundational literacy and numeracy for every child by the end of Class 3.
  1. Launch place: Panipat (BBBP), Ballia (Ujjwala), Ranchi (PM-JAY), Jhunjhunu (POSHAN Abhiyaan) — asked again and again.
  2. Confusing pairs: POSHAN Abhiyaan (nutrition mission) vs PM POSHAN (school meals).
  3. Coverage numbers: ₹5 lakh per family, 70+ under Vay Vandana, ₹5,000 under Matru Vandana.

💡 Explanation: PM-JAY offers ₹5 lakh per family per year for hospital care.

📌 Prelims Traps to Avoid

  • ✘ PM-JAY covers hospitalisation, not outpatient visits.
  • ✘ POSHAN Abhiyaan is under Women and Child Development; PM POSHAN is under Education.
  • ✘ Ujjwala is run by the Petroleum ministry, not Women and Child Development.

Who is eligible for the Ayushman Vay Vandana card?

All Indian citizens aged 70 and above, regardless of income, have been eligible since October 2024. It provides health cover of up to ₹5 lakh a year under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.

What is the difference between POSHAN Abhiyaan and PM POSHAN?

POSHAN Abhiyaan (2018) is the National Nutrition Mission for children under six, pregnant women and lactating mothers, run by the Ministry of Women and Child Development. PM POSHAN (2021) is the renamed Mid-Day Meal scheme for schoolchildren, run by the Ministry of Education.
