🌾 Agriculture & Rural Development Schemes

PM-KISAN • PMFBY • Soil Health Card • PMKSY • e-NAM • Rural Jobs • Housing • Water

High Yield Schemes 📅 Last updated: Oct 2026
  • Agriculture supports nearly half of India's workforce, so farm and rural schemes are tested in almost every exam, especially UPSC, State PSCs and NABARD/RBI papers.
  • Most farm schemes are run by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare; most rural schemes by the Ministry of Rural Development.
  • Learn each scheme with its tagline — exams often ask "'Har Khet Ko Pani' is the motto of which scheme?".

Soil, Water and Markets

  • Soil Health Card (February 19, 2015, Suratgarh, Rajasthan) — tests soil and recommends nutrients and fertiliser doses for each farm.
  • PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (2015) — motto "Har Khet Ko Pani" (water to every field) and "More Crop Per Drop" (micro-irrigation).
  • e-NAM (April 14, 2016) — an online trading platform linking regulated mandis (APMCs) across states; run by the Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium.
  • Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (2015) — promotes organic farming through farmer clusters.
  • Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (2020) — ₹1 lakh crore financing facility for post-harvest infrastructure such as cold storage and warehouses.

Rural Employment

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (2005) guaranteed 100 days of wage work a year to every rural household whose adults volunteer for unskilled manual work. It has been replaced by the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025, which raises the guarantee to 125 days a year. Check the latest notification for implementation details.

Rural Livelihoods

  • Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM), launched in June 2011 as "Aajeevika", organises poor rural women into self-help groups and links them to bank credit.
  • Its Lakhpati Didi initiative aims to help SHG women earn at least ₹1 lakh a year; the target is 3 crore women.
  • Namo Drone Didi (2023) gives drones to women SHGs to provide agricultural services such as spraying fertiliser.

Q1. Under PMFBY, the farmer's premium for rabi food and oilseed crops is:

✅ Answer: (b) 1.5%

💡 Explanation: Farmers pay 2% for kharif, 1.5% for rabi and 5% for commercial and horticultural crops.

Q2. "Har Khet Ko Pani" is the motto of:

✅ Answer: (a) PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana

💡 Explanation: PMKSY aims to expand irrigation to every field.

Q3. The Jal Jeevan Mission aims to provide:

✅ Answer: (d) A tap water connection to every rural household

💡 Explanation: Its goal is a functional household tap connection — "Har Ghar Jal".

Q4. PM-KISAN provides eligible farmer families with:

✅ Answer: (b) ₹6,000 a year in three instalments

💡 Explanation: Three instalments of ₹2,000 are paid directly into bank accounts.

Q5. The SVAMITVA scheme uses drones to:

✅ Answer: (c) Map village residential land and issue property cards

💡 Explanation: Launched on April 24, 2020, it gives rural households a legal record of their property.

🔁 Revise in 60 Seconds

  • PM-KISAN 2019: ₹6,000/yr in 3 × ₹2,000.
  • PMFBY 2016: 2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% commercial; voluntary since 2020.
  • Soil Health Card 2015 (Suratgarh); PMKSY 2015 (Har Khet Ko Pani); e-NAM 2016.
  • JJM 2019 (Har Ghar Jal); PMAY-G 2016; SBM-G 2014; DAY-NRLM 2011; SVAMITVA 2020.

📌 Prelims Traps to Avoid

  • ✘ PM-KISAN is 100% centrally funded; PMFBY premiums are shared by the Centre and states.
  • ✘ Jal Jeevan Mission is about drinking water, not irrigation.
  • ✘ e-NAM links existing mandis online; it does not replace them.

What is the farmer's premium under PM Fasal Bima Yojana?

2% of the sum insured for kharif crops, 1.5% for rabi crops and 5% for annual commercial and horticultural crops. The remaining premium is paid by the Centre and the states.

How much money do farmers get under PM-KISAN?

₹6,000 a year per eligible farmer family, paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 directly into their bank accounts.
