🏗️ Industry, Infrastructure & Digital Schemes

Make in India • PLI • Startup India • Gati Shakti • UDAN • Smart Cities • Digital India

High Yield Schemes 📅 Last updated: Oct 2026
  • These schemes appear in UPSC GS Paper 3 (economy and infrastructure) and as dates and objectives in SSC and bank papers.
  • Several were launched together: Smart Cities, AMRUT and PMAY-Urban all on June 25, 2015.
  • Watch for full forms — UDAN is "Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik", AMRUT is "Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation".

Urban Development

  • Smart Cities Mission — launched June 25, 2015; 100 cities chosen through a competition.
  • AMRUT — launched the same day for water supply, sewerage and green spaces in 500 cities; AMRUT 2.0 (2021) aims at water-secure cities.
  • PMAY-Urban — also June 25, 2015; PMAY-U 2.0 was approved in 2024 for 1 crore more urban houses.

Digital and Energy

  • Digital India (July 1, 2015) has three vision areas: digital infrastructure as a core utility, governance and services on demand, and digital empowerment of citizens. It includes DigiLocker, BharatNet (broadband for gram panchayats) and Common Service Centres.
  • PM-WANI (2020) — public Wi-Fi through local data offices.
  • PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (February 2024) — subsidies for rooftop solar so that 1 crore households can get up to 300 units of free electricity a month.
  • PM-KUSUM (2019) — solar pumps and grid-connected solar plants for farmers.
  • National Green Hydrogen Mission (January 2023) — target of 5 million tonnes of green hydrogen a year by 2030; see our detailed notes.
  1. Same-day launches: Smart Cities, AMRUT and PMAY-U (June 25, 2015).
  2. Full forms: UDAN, AMRUT, PM-KUSUM, PM-WANI.
  3. Numbers: 14 PLI sectors, 18 Vishwakarma trades, 100 smart cities, 300 free units under Surya Ghar.

Q1. PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan was launched on:

✅ Answer: (a) October 13, 2021

💡 Explanation: It was launched on October 13, 2021, as a GIS-based platform for infrastructure planning.

Q2. The first flight under the UDAN scheme flew between:

✅ Answer: (c) Shimla and Delhi

💡 Explanation: The first UDAN flight was Shimla–Delhi on April 27, 2017.

Q3. How many traditional trades are covered under PM Vishwakarma?

✅ Answer: (d) 18

💡 Explanation: The scheme covers 18 trades, including carpenters, blacksmiths, potters and tailors.

Q4. Which of these was NOT launched on June 25, 2015?

✅ Answer: (b) Digital India

💡 Explanation: Digital India was launched on July 1, 2015.

Q5. PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana aims to provide free electricity of up to how many units a month?

✅ Answer: (a) 300

💡 Explanation: It subsidises rooftop solar for 1 crore households to get up to 300 free units a month.

📌 Prelims Traps to Avoid

  • ✘ UDAN stands for Ude Desh ka Aam Nagrik, not a transport ministry acronym.
  • ✘ Make in India is run by DPIIT, not the MSME ministry.
  • ✘ PLI pays on incremental sales, not on investment alone.

Which schemes were launched on June 25, 2015?

The Smart Cities Mission, AMRUT (Atal Mission for Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation) and Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Urban.

What is PM Gati Shakti?

A National Master Plan launched on October 13, 2021, that uses a GIS-based digital platform to bring together more than 16 ministries, including railways and roads, for integrated planning of infrastructure projects.
