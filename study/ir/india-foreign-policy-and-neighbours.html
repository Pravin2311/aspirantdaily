🧭 India's Foreign Policy & Neighbourhood

Panchsheel • Non-Alignment • Act East • Neighbourhood First • SAGAR • Borders

High Yield International Relations 📅 Last updated: Oct 2026
🔍 Quick Search: Panchsheel five principles 1954, Gujral doctrine meaning, Look East to Act East year, SAGAR full form, India longest land border, McMahon vs Radcliffe vs Durand line
  • India's foreign policy has moved from non-alignment during the Cold War to what is now described as strategic autonomy and "multi-alignment" — working with many partners without joining military alliances.
  • Its core ideas appear in the Constitution: Article 51 (a Directive Principle) asks the State to promote international peace and security and respect for international law.
  • India shares land borders with seven countries — Bangladesh, China, Pakistan, Nepal, Myanmar, Bhutan and Afghanistan — totalling about 15,106.7 km.

Q1. The Gujral Doctrine is associated with:

✅ Answer: (a) Non-reciprocal goodwill towards smaller neighbours

💡 Explanation: It held that India should give more to neighbours without demanding reciprocity.

Q2. India shares its longest land border with:

✅ Answer: (d) Bangladesh

💡 Explanation: The India–Bangladesh border is about 4,096.7 km long.

Q3. The McMahon Line separates India from:

✅ Answer: (b) China

💡 Explanation: It marks the eastern sector of the India–China boundary, drawn at the Simla Convention of 1914.

Q4. Which state does NOT share a border with Myanmar?

✅ Answer: (c) Assam

💡 Explanation: The Myanmar border touches Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur and Mizoram.

Q5. The SAGAR vision for the Indian Ocean was announced in 2015 in:

✅ Answer: (d) Mauritius

💡 Explanation: PM Modi outlined SAGAR in Mauritius in March 2015; MAHASAGAR was also announced there in 2025.

📌 Prelims Traps to Avoid

  • ✘ Panchsheel was signed with China, not at the NAM summit.
  • ✘ The Durand Line does not touch Indian-administered territory.
  • ✘ Look East (1991) was upgraded to Act East in 2014 — they are not separate policies.

What are the five principles of Panchsheel?

Mutual respect for each other's territorial integrity and sovereignty, mutual non-aggression, mutual non-interference in internal affairs, equality and mutual benefit, and peaceful coexistence. They were set out in the India–China agreement of April 29, 1954.

What is the difference between Look East and Act East?

Look East (1991) focused mainly on economic ties with South-East Asia. Act East (2014) widened this to security, connectivity and culture and extended it to the wider Indo-Pacific.
