Animal Kingdom Classification: Eleven Phyla and Seven Vertebrate Classes with Key Features and Examples

Porifera • Coelenterata • Ctenophora • Platyhelminthes • Aschelminthes • Annelida • Arthropoda (largest) • Mollusca (second largest) • Echinodermata • Hemichordata • Chordata

SSC Railway 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01

More than a million animal species have been described, so NCERT sorts them by a few shared features. Knowing these terms makes the phylum table below easy to read.

BasisTypesExamples
Level of organisationCellular, tissue, organ, organ systemSponges (cellular), coelenterates (tissue), flatworms (organ), annelids to chordates (organ system)
SymmetryAsymmetrical, radial, bilateralSponges; coelenterates, ctenophores and echinoderms (radial); annelids and arthropods (bilateral)
Germ layersDiploblastic (ectoderm and endoderm, with mesoglea between), triploblastic (adds mesoderm)Coelenterates; flatworms to chordates
Body cavityCoelomate, pseudocoelomate, acoelomateAnnelids, molluscs, arthropods, echinoderms, hemichordates, chordates; roundworms; flatworms
SegmentationMetameric segmentation (metamerism)Earthworm
NotochordChordates have one; non-chordates do notPorifera to echinoderms are non-chordates

The digestive system can be incomplete, with one opening as in flatworms, or complete, with a mouth and anus. Circulation can be open, where blood bathes the tissues directly, or closed, where it flows through vessels.

NCERT groups animals by their level of organisation, symmetry, germ layers, body cavity, segmentation and notochord. These are the ten non-chordate phyla, with the examples exams ask about.

HemichordataWorm-like and marine; stomochord in the collar; once a chordate subphylum, now a separate phylumBalanoglossus, Saccoglossus

Watch the names: the "fish" names are traps. Starfish (Asterias) is an echinoderm, cuttlefish (Sepia) is a mollusc, and jellyfish (Aurelia) is a coelenterate. None of them are fish.

Chordata has three subphyla. In Urochordata (Ascidia, Salpa, Doliolum), the notochord is present only in the larval tail. In Cephalochordata (Branchiostoma, also called Amphioxus), it runs from head to tail for life. These two are the protochordates, and both are marine. The third subphylum is Vertebrata.

Quick heart-chamber rule: fish have two chambers, amphibians and most reptiles three, and crocodiles, birds and mammals four.

Q1. Which is the largest phylum of the animal kingdom? easy

✅ Answer: (c) Arthropoda

💡 Explanation: Arthropoda is the largest phylum; over two-thirds of all named species are arthropods. Mollusca is second.

Q2. Limulus (king crab) is called a living fossil. It belongs to: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Arthropoda

💡 Explanation: NCERT lists Limulus, the king crab, under Arthropoda as a living fossil, along with honey bee, silkworm and locust.

Q3. A water vascular system is the most distinctive feature of: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Echinodermata

💡 Explanation: Echinoderms use their water vascular system for locomotion, feeding and respiration.

Q4. Which reptile has a four-chambered heart? medium

✅ Answer: (b) Crocodile

💡 Explanation: Reptile hearts are usually three-chambered, but crocodiles have four chambers.

Q5. Flame cells for excretion are found in: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Flatworms

💡 Explanation: Platyhelminthes such as Taenia and Fasciola use flame cells for osmoregulation and excretion. Annelids use nephridia.

Which phylum is the second largest?

Mollusca, with examples such as Pila (apple snail), Pinctada (pearl oyster), Sepia (cuttlefish), Loligo (squid) and Octopus. Arthropoda is the largest.

Is Hemichordata a chordate group?

Not any more. It was once treated as a subphylum of Chordata, but NCERT now places it as a separate non-chordate phylum, with a stomochord rather than a true notochord.
