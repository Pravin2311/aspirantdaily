NCERT presents common salt as the starting point for several everyday chemicals:

  • Caustic soda: passing electricity through brine gives sodium hydroxide, chlorine and hydrogen. This is the chlor-alkali process, named after chlorine and alkali. Chlorine forms at the anode and hydrogen at the cathode.
  • Bleaching powder: made by the action of chlorine on dry slaked lime.
  • Baking soda: produced using sodium chloride as one raw material. It is a mild, non-corrosive basic salt.
  • Washing soda: heating baking soda gives sodium carbonate; recrystallising it gives washing soda.

Rock salt is the large brownish crystals of salt found in deposits left when ancient seas dried up. It is mined like coal.

Water of crystallisation is the fixed number of water molecules in one formula unit of a salt:

  • copper sulphate: 5 (CuSO4.5H2O);
  • washing soda: 10 (Na2CO3.10H2O);
  • gypsum: 2 (CaSO4.2H2O);
  • Plaster of Paris: ½ (CaSO4.½H2O). Mixed with water, it sets back into gypsum as a hard solid mass.

  • Baking powder is not baking soda. It is a mixture of baking soda and tartaric acid, a weak edible acid.
  • Glacial acetic acid gets its name because pure ethanoic acid melts at 290 K, so it often freezes in cold winters.
  • Bleaching powder is only represented as Ca(ClO)2; NCERT notes its actual composition is quite complex.

Q1. The chemical name of baking soda is: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Sodium hydrogencarbonate

💡 Explanation: NCERT gives baking soda as sodium hydrogencarbonate, NaHCO3, a mild non-corrosive basic salt.

Q2. Washing soda contains how many molecules of water of crystallisation? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 10

💡 Explanation: Washing soda is Na2CO3.10H2O; copper sulphate has 5 and gypsum has 2.

Q3. Plaster of Paris is obtained by heating which substance at 373 K? easy

✅ Answer: (c) Gypsum

💡 Explanation: Gypsum loses water at 373 K to give calcium sulphate hemihydrate, Plaster of Paris.

Q4. Bleaching powder is made by the action of chlorine on: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Dry slaked lime

💡 Explanation: NCERT says bleaching powder is produced by chlorine acting on dry slaked lime, Ca(OH)2.

Q5. Solder is an alloy of: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Lead and tin

💡 Explanation: Solder is lead and tin with a low melting point, used to weld electrical wires.

Q6. Aqua regia is a mixture of conc. HCl and conc. HNO3 in the ratio: medium

✅ Answer: (b) 3:1

💡 Explanation: NCERT gives the ratio of hydrochloric acid to nitric acid as 3:1.

Q7. Vinegar is a solution of acetic acid in water of strength: easy

✅ Answer: (c) 5-8%

💡 Explanation: NCERT says a 5-8% solution of acetic acid in water is called vinegar.

Q8. In the chlor-alkali process, hydrogen gas is released at the: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Cathode

💡 Explanation: Chlorine is given off at the anode, hydrogen at the cathode, and NaOH forms near the cathode.

What is the difference between baking soda and baking powder?

Baking soda is sodium hydrogencarbonate. Baking powder is a mixture of baking soda and tartaric acid, which releases carbon dioxide to make cakes soft and spongy.

Why is it called glacial acetic acid?

Pure ethanoic acid melts at 290 K, so it often freezes during winter in cold climates. NCERT says this gave rise to the name glacial acetic acid.
