General Science Biology MCQs: 30 Questions on the Cell, Tissues and Classification With Explained Answers

Hooke, Schleiden, Schwann, Virchow • Prokaryotes vs eukaryotes • Organelles and plastids • Meristems • Xylem and phloem • Muscles and joints • Whittaker and Linnaeus • Hotspots

SSC Railways 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-06

These 30 original questions cover three biology chapters of NCERT's 2026-27 Class 9 science textbook "Exploration": the cell (chapter 2), tissues, muscles and joints (chapter 3), and diversity and classification (chapter 12). Questions 1 to 10 are on the cell, 11 to 20 on tissues, and 21 to 30 on classification.

Many of these questions turn on names and pairs: who discovered what, which organelle does what, which tissue sits where. The table below collects the pairs most likely to be confused.

Q1. The cell was first observed in 1665 by: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Robert Hooke

💡 Explanation: Hooke observed cells in 1665 using a microscope he designed himself.

Q2. That all plants are made of cells was reported in 1838 by: easy

✅ Answer: (b) Matthias Schleiden

💡 Explanation: Schleiden, a German botanist, reported in 1838 that all plants are made up of cells.

Q3. 'New cells are formed only from pre-existing cells' was stated by: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Rudolf Virchow

💡 Explanation: Virchow expanded the Cell Theory in 1855 with this statement.

Q4. Cells without a well-defined nucleus are called: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Prokaryotic

💡 Explanation: Bacteria lack a well-defined nucleus and membrane-bound organelles, so they are prokaryotic.

Q5. The region holding DNA in a prokaryotic cell is the: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Nucleoid

💡 Explanation: Prokaryotic DNA is a single circular molecule lying in a region called the nucleoid.

Q6. Which organelle is called the powerhouse of the cell? easy

✅ Answer: (b) Mitochondria

💡 Explanation: Mitochondria supply the energy needed for most of the cell's activities, NCERT explains.

Q7. Rough endoplasmic reticulum looks rough because it has: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Ribosomes on its surface

💡 Explanation: Ribosomes attached to RER give it a rough look; it works in protein synthesis.

Q8. Plastids that store starch in potato cells are: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Leucoplasts

💡 Explanation: Colourless leucoplasts store food such as starch, oils or proteins.

Q9. The Golgi apparatus was first observed in 1898 by: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Camillo Golgi

💡 Explanation: Golgi saw it in the nerve cells of a barn owl using special stains.

Q10. Functional segments of DNA are called: easy

✅ Answer: (b) Genes

💡 Explanation: NCERT defines genes as the functional segments of DNA carrying genetic information.

Q11. Growth in length of roots and shoots is due to the: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Apical meristem

💡 Explanation: Dividing cells at root and shoot tips form the apical meristems.

Q12. Which tissue has thick walls with lignin and mostly dead cells? easy

✅ Answer: (d) Sclerenchyma

💡 Explanation: Lignin deposition makes sclerenchyma hard, as in coconut husk and walnut shells.

Q13. Stems and tendrils bend without breaking because of: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Collenchyma

💡 Explanation: Collenchyma has living cells with corners thickened by pectin, giving flexible support.

Q14. Water and minerals are carried in plants by: easy

✅ Answer: (b) Xylem

💡 Explanation: Xylem carries water and minerals from the roots to other parts of the plant.

Q15. Loading and unloading of sugars in sieve tubes is monitored by: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Companion cells

💡 Explanation: Companion cells are specialised parenchyma cells that regulate sieve tube function.

Q16. A ligament connects: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Bone to bone

💡 Explanation: Ligaments join bones, give stability and limit movement; tendons join muscle to bone.

Q17. Muscles that are branched, involuntary and found only in the heart are: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Cardiac muscles

💡 Explanation: Cardiac fibres are branched with one nucleus and work without fatigue.

Q18. Smooth muscle cells are: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Spindle-shaped with one nucleus

💡 Explanation: Smooth muscle cells are spindle-shaped, have one nucleus and no striations.

Q19. The shoulder is an example of a: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Ball and socket joint

💡 Explanation: The rounded top of the arm bone fits a hollow in the shoulder, allowing movement in all directions.

Q20. The number of pairs of ribs in humans is: easy

✅ Answer: (d) 12

💡 Explanation: NCERT: we have 12 pairs of ribs, which together form the rib cage.

Q21. The five kingdom classification was proposed by: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Whittaker

💡 Explanation: Whittaker's 1969 system grouped life into Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.

Q22. Binomial nomenclature was introduced by: easy

✅ Answer: (b) Carolus Linnaeus

💡 Explanation: Linnaeus introduced two-part scientific names, genus then species, in the 18th century.

Q23. In Panthera tigris, 'Panthera' is the: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Genus

💡 Explanation: The first word of a binomial name is the genus and the second is the species.

Q24. Bacteria belong to the kingdom: easy

✅ Answer: (d) Monera

💡 Explanation: Kingdom Monera holds unicellular prokaryotes such as bacteria, which lack a true nucleus.

Q25. Amoeba and Paramecium belong to the kingdom: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Protista

💡 Explanation: Kingdom Protista includes unicellular eukaryotes like these, which have a true nucleus.

Q26. Which plant group is called the 'amphibians' of the plant kingdom? medium

✅ Answer: (b) Bryophyta

💡 Explanation: Bryophytes grow on land but still need water for reproduction, like amphibians.

Q27. Which organisms were among the first to produce oxygen by photosynthesis? medium

✅ Answer: (a) Cyanobacteria

💡 Explanation: Cyanobacteria released oxygen that built up about 2.5 billion years ago.

Q28. The three domain system was proposed in 1977 by: hard

✅ Answer: (d) Carl Woese

💡 Explanation: Woese used genetic data to propose the three domain system in 1977.

Q29. Regions rich in endemic species that have suffered significant habitat loss are called: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Biodiversity hotspots

💡 Explanation: NCERT names the Western Ghats, Indo-Burma, the Himalayas and Sundaland as examples.

Q30. Grouping animals by habitat on land, in water and in air was done by: medium

✅ Answer: (b) Aristotle

💡 Explanation: Aristotle grouped animals this way around the 4th century BCE.

Which chapters does this set cover?

Chapters 2, 3 and 12 of the 2026-27 NCERT Class 9 book: the cell, tissues with muscles and joints, and diversity and classification. Ten questions come from each chapter.

Who built the cell theory?

Schleiden showed in 1838 that plants are made of cells, Schwann in 1839 that animals are, and Virchow in 1855 that new cells come only from existing cells.
