⚗️ Chemistry in Everyday Life

pH & Acids • Common Compounds • Ores • Alloys • Polymers • Fuels

High Yield Science & Tech 📅 Last updated: Oct 2026
  • Chemistry questions in SSC and RRB papers lean heavily on common names and formulas, ores and alloys — almost pure memory, so they are quick marks.
  • The pH scale runs from 0 to 14: below 7 is acidic, 7 is neutral and above 7 is basic. Each step is a tenfold change.
  • Air is about 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 0.93% argon and about 0.04% carbon dioxide.

Typical pH values: stomach acid about 1.5–3.5; pure water 7; human blood about 7.4 (slightly basic). Tooth decay starts when the pH in the mouth falls below about 5.5. An ant sting is soothed by a mild base such as baking soda.

Record-Holding Elements

  • Mercury is the only metal liquid at room temperature; bromine the only liquid non-metal.
  • Hydrogen is the lightest element; lithium the lightest metal.
  • Tungsten has the highest melting point of all metals; silver is the best conductor of heat and electricity; gold is the most malleable and ductile.
  • In the Earth's crust, oxygen is the most abundant element and aluminium the most abundant metal.

Polymers and Fuels

  • Teflon (PTFE) makes non-stick coatings; PVC pipes and cables; nylon was the first fully synthetic fibre; Bakelite is a thermosetting plastic used in electrical switches because it does not soften on heating.
  • Natural rubber is a polymer of isoprene; vulcanisation with sulphur (Charles Goodyear) makes it tougher.
  • LPG is mainly butane with propane; a strong-smelling compound, ethyl mercaptan, is added so leaks can be detected. CNG and biogas are mainly methane.
  • Petrol quality is rated by its octane number, diesel by its cetane number.
  • Use CO₂ or dry-powder extinguishers on oil and electrical fires — water spreads burning oil and conducts electricity.
  1. Common name ↔ formula — especially the calcium compounds (gypsum vs plaster of Paris, quicklime vs slaked lime).
  2. Ore ↔ metal and alloy ↔ composition, often as "match the following".
  3. Odd facts: "German silver contains…" (no silver), "Which is a liquid non-metal?" (bromine).

Q1. The chemical formula of baking soda is:

✅ Answer: (b) NaHCO₃

💡 Explanation: Baking soda is sodium hydrogen carbonate, NaHCO₃.

Q2. Bauxite is the main ore of:

✅ Answer: (c) Aluminium

💡 Explanation: Aluminium is extracted from bauxite.

Q3. Which acid is present in an ant sting?

✅ Answer: (a) Formic acid

💡 Explanation: Ants and bees inject formic (methanoic) acid.

Q4. Brass is an alloy of:

✅ Answer: (d) Copper and zinc

💡 Explanation: Brass is copper and zinc; bronze is copper and tin.

Q5. The main component of CNG is:

✅ Answer: (a) Methane

💡 Explanation: Compressed natural gas is mostly methane.

🔁 Revise in 60 Seconds

  • Lemon citric, vinegar acetic, tamarind tartaric, tomato oxalic, curd lactic, ant formic.
  • NaHCO₃ baking soda; Na₂CO₃·10H₂O washing soda; CaOCl₂ bleaching powder; CaSO₄·½H₂O plaster of Paris.
  • Bauxite → Al, haematite → Fe, galena → Pb, cinnabar → Hg.
  • Brass Cu+Zn, bronze Cu+Sn, solder Pb+Sn; LPG butane, CNG methane.

📌 Prelims Traps to Avoid

  • ✘ Gypsum has 2 water molecules; plaster of Paris has ½.
  • ✘ German silver contains no silver.
  • ✘ Pure LPG is odourless — the smell comes from added ethyl mercaptan.

What is the chemical name of washing soda?

Sodium carbonate decahydrate, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O. Baking soda is a different compound: sodium hydrogen carbonate, NaHCO₃.

Which acid is found in tamarind?

Tartaric acid. Tomatoes contain oxalic acid, lemons citric acid and vinegar acetic acid.
