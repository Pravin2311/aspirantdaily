🧬 Cell, Genetics & Plant Biology

Cell Organelles • Mitosis vs Meiosis • DNA • Mendel • Genetic Disorders • Photosynthesis • Plant Hormones

High Yield Science & Tech 📅 Last updated: Oct 2026
  • Biology is the largest part of general science in SSC and railway papers, and a large share of those questions come from cell biology, genetics and plants.
  • Most questions are one-liners: "Which organelle is the powerhouse of the cell?", "Which hormone ripens fruit?", "Down syndrome is caused by…?" — so a clean set of matched pairs goes a long way.
  • This page complements our Human Body & Health notes, which cover body systems, vitamins and diseases.

Discovery of the Cell

  • Robert Hooke first observed (dead) cells in cork in 1665; Antonie van Leeuwenhoek first saw living cells.
  • Robert Brown discovered the nucleus in 1831.
  • Cell theory — Schleiden (plants, 1838) and Schwann (animals, 1839); Rudolf Virchow added that all cells come from pre-existing cells (1855).

DNA, Genes and Chromosomes

  • The double-helix structure of DNA was described by Watson and Crick in 1953, using X-ray images by Rosalind Franklin.
  • DNA bases pair as A–T and G–C; RNA has uracil instead of thymine.
  • Humans have 46 chromosomes (23 pairs) — 22 pairs of autosomes and one pair of sex chromosomes. Females are XX and males XY, so the father's sperm decides the sex of the child.

Mendel's Laws

Gregor Mendel, the "father of genetics", experimented on pea plants. His three laws are dominance, segregation and independent assortment. A monohybrid cross gives a 3:1 phenotypic ratio (1:2:1 genotypic); a dihybrid cross gives 9:3:3:1.

Classification of Living Things

Carolus Linnaeus introduced binomial nomenclature (genus + species, e.g., Homo sapiens) and is the father of taxonomy. R.H. Whittaker proposed the five-kingdom system (1969): Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia.

Plants

  • Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂, in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll. Light reactions take place in the thylakoids; the Calvin cycle in the stroma. The oxygen released comes from water.
  • Xylem carries water and minerals upward; phloem carries food. Water is lost through stomata by transpiration.
  • Nitrogen fixation: Rhizobium bacteria in the root nodules of legumes convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms.

Q1. Which organelle is known as the "suicide bag" of the cell?

✅ Answer: (c) Lysosome

💡 Explanation: Lysosomes contain digestive enzymes that can break down the cell itself.

Q2. Down syndrome is caused by:

✅ Answer: (a) An extra chromosome 21

💡 Explanation: Down syndrome is trisomy 21; Turner is XO and Klinefelter is XXY.

Q3. Which plant hormone is a gas and helps ripen fruit?

✅ Answer: (d) Ethylene

💡 Explanation: Ethylene is the only gaseous plant hormone.

Q4. Meiosis produces:

✅ Answer: (b) Four haploid cells

💡 Explanation: Meiosis halves the chromosome number and forms four genetically different gametes.

Q5. The five-kingdom classification was proposed by:

✅ Answer: (c) R.H. Whittaker

💡 Explanation: Whittaker proposed Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae and Animalia in 1969.

🔁 Revise in 60 Seconds

  • Mitochondria — powerhouse; lysosome — suicide bag; ribosome — protein; chloroplast & cell wall — plants.
  • Mitosis: 2 identical; meiosis: 4 haploid. 46 chromosomes; father decides sex. Mendel 3:1, 9:3:3:1.

📌 Prelims Traps to Avoid

  • ✘ Ribosomes are found in both prokaryotes and eukaryotes, and have no membrane.
  • ✘ The oxygen released in photosynthesis comes from water, not carbon dioxide.
  • ✘ Fungal cell walls are made of chitin, not cellulose.

What is the difference between mitosis and meiosis?

Mitosis takes place in body cells and produces two identical diploid daughter cells for growth and repair. Meiosis takes place in reproductive cells and produces four genetically different haploid cells (gametes) for sexual reproduction.

Which organelle is called the powerhouse of the cell?

The mitochondrion, because it produces ATP — the cell's energy currency — through cellular respiration. Mitochondria also have their own DNA.
