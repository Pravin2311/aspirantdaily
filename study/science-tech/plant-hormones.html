Plant Hormones and Their Functions: Auxin, Gibberellin, Cytokinin, Ethylene and Abscisic Acid

Promoters: auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins • Inhibitors: abscisic acid, ethylene (mostly) • Discoveries: Went, Kurosawa, Skoog and Miller, Cousins • Ethephon • 2,4-D

SSC Railway 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01

Plant growth regulators (PGRs), also called plant hormones or phytohormones, are small, simple molecules. NCERT sorts them into two groups by function:

  • Growth promoters: auxins, gibberellins and cytokinins. They drive cell division and enlargement, tropic growth, flowering, fruiting and seed formation.
  • Inhibitors and stress responders: abscisic acid. These control dormancy and abscission and respond to wounds and stress.
  • Ethylene could fit either group, but NCERT calls it largely an inhibitor.

Auxins (Greek auxein, to grow) are made at the growing tips of stems and roots. NCERT notes that auxin was first isolated from human urine. IAA and IBA occur in plants, while NAA and 2,4-D are synthetic.

  • Start roots on stem cuttings, which is used in plant propagation.
  • Promote flowering in pineapple.
  • Prevent early fruit and leaf drop, but promote the shedding of old leaves and fruits.
  • Cause apical dominance: the top bud suppresses the side buds. Cutting off the tip lets side branches grow, which is why tea bushes and hedges are pruned.
  • Induce parthenocarpy (seedless fruit) in tomato.
  • 2,4-D kills dicot weeds but spares mature monocots, so it is used for weed-free lawns.
  • Control xylem differentiation and help in cell division.

Gibberellins: there are more than 100 of them, all acidic, and GA3 is the best studied.

  • Lengthen grape stalks, and elongate apples and improve their shape.
  • Delay senescence, so fruit can stay longer on the tree.
  • Speed up malting in brewing.
  • Lengthen sugarcane stems, raising yield by as much as 20 tonnes per acre.
  • Make young conifers mature early.
  • Cause bolting in beet and cabbage.

Cytokinins: kinetin came from autoclaved herring sperm DNA and does not occur naturally in plants. Zeatin, from corn kernels and coconut milk, is a natural cytokinin. Cytokinins are made where cells divide fast, such as root tips, young buds and fruits.

  • Help form new leaves and chloroplasts.
  • Promote lateral shoots and adventitious shoots.
  • Overcome apical dominance.
  • Delay leaf senescence by moving nutrients.

Ethylene is made in large amounts by ripening fruits and ageing tissues.

  • Ripens fruit and raises its respiration rate during ripening (the respiratory climacteric).
  • Speeds the ageing and shedding of leaves and flowers.
  • Breaks seed and bud dormancy and sprouts potato tubers.
  • Makes deep-water rice elongate so its leaves stay above water.
  • Promotes root hairs.
  • Starts flowering in pineapple and mango.

Farmers apply it as ethephon, which releases ethylene slowly. Ethephon ripens tomatoes and apples, thins cotton, cherry and walnut, and promotes female flowers in cucumber.

Abscisic acid is a general growth inhibitor.

  • Inhibits seed germination.
  • Closes stomata and raises tolerance to stress, which is why it is called the stress hormone.
  • Induces seed dormancy, so seeds survive drying out.
  • In most situations, works against gibberellins.

Several events, such as seed dormancy, abscission, senescence and apical dominance, involve more than one hormone working together. Related notes: photosynthesis and respiration and cell, genetics and plant biology.

Q1. Which plant hormone is called the stress hormone? easy

✅ Answer: (c) Abscisic acid

💡 Explanation: Abscisic acid closes stomata and raises tolerance to stresses, so NCERT says it is also called the stress hormone.

Q2. The 'bakanae' (foolish seedling) disease of rice led to the discovery of: easy

✅ Answer: (a) Gibberellins

💡 Explanation: The fungus Gibberella fujikuroi caused bakanae disease, and the active substance was later identified as gibberellic acid.

Q3. Which hormone helps overcome apical dominance? medium

✅ Answer: (d) Cytokinin

💡 Explanation: Auxin from the apical bud causes apical dominance. Cytokinins help overcome it and promote lateral shoot growth.

Q4. Ethephon is used in farming because it: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Releases ethylene slowly

💡 Explanation: Ethephon is the most widely used source of ethylene. It is absorbed and releases ethylene slowly, which hastens ripening and promotes female flowers in cucumber.

Q5. 2,4-D is a synthetic form of which hormone? easy

✅ Answer: (b) Auxin

💡 Explanation: NAA and 2,4-D are synthetic auxins. 2,4-D is used as a herbicide against dicot weeds.

Q6. Bolting (internode elongation just before flowering) in cabbage is promoted by: medium

✅ Answer: (c) Gibberellins

💡 Explanation: Gibberellins promote bolting in beet, cabbage and other rosette plants.

Which plant hormone is a gas?

Ethylene (C2H4) is the only gaseous plant growth regulator. It is made in large amounts by ripening fruits and ageing tissues, which is why one ripe fruit speeds up the ripening of others nearby.

Which hormones promote growth and which inhibit it?

Auxins, gibberellins and cytokinins are growth promoters. Abscisic acid is a growth inhibitor. NCERT says ethylene could fit either group but is largely an inhibitor.
