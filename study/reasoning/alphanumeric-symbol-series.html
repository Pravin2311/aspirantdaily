Alphanumeric Series Reasoning: Position From Left and Right, Element Counting and Removal Questions

One series, many questions • Right-to-left conversion: n − k + 1 • Moving left or right • Counting triplet patterns • Removing elements • Middle element

Banking 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-02

Alphanumeric series questions give one long mix of letters, numbers and symbols, followed by five or six questions on it. Every example on this page uses the same series of 24 elements:

R 4 # B 7 $ K M 2 @ E 9 % T 3 & P 1 * D 5 H + 8

Before answering anything, do two things. First, count the elements and write the total (n = 24) above the series. Second, mark every fifth position lightly with a dot so you can count quickly. This one-minute setup saves time on every question that follows.

Worked example 1: what the series contains

The series has 9 letters, 8 numbers and 7 symbols. Their sum, 8 + 7 + 9 = 24, matches the length you counted, which confirms nothing was missed.

The k-th element from the right is the (n − k + 1)-th from the left. Converting every right-side position to a left-side one avoids counting in two directions.

k-th from right = (n − k + 1)-th from left

Worked example 2: 5th from the right

24 − 5 + 1 = 20, so the 5th from the right is the 20th from the left: D.

"To the right of" adds positions; "to the left of" subtracts them.

Worked example 3: moving along the series

The element 4th to the right of the 10th from the left is at position 10 + 4 = 14: T.

Worked example 4: mixing both directions

The element 3rd to the left of the 8th from the right: the 8th from the right is position 24 − 8 + 1 = 17, and three to its left is 17 − 3 = 14, again T.

Worked example 5: the middle element

Which element is midway between the 6th from the left and the 9th from the right? The 9th from the right is position 16. The middle of positions 6 and 16 is (6 + 16) ÷ 2 = 11: E. If the two positions add to an odd number, there is no single middle element.

Questions like "how many numbers are immediately preceded by a symbol and followed by a letter?" ask you to slide a small pattern along the series.

  1. Write the pattern in type form, for example symbol – number – letter.
  2. Find every element of the middle type (here, every number).
  3. For each one, check only its two neighbours.

Starting from the middle type is quicker than checking every triplet. In the practice series, the pattern number – symbol – letter appears six times: 4#B, 7$K, 2@E, 9%T, 3&P and 1*D. The + near the end fails because it follows H, a letter.

Two-element patterns work the same way. Letters immediately followed by a number occur seven times: R4, B7, M2, E9, T3, P1 and D5.

Watch the ends of the series. The first element has nothing before it and the last has nothing after it, so they can never be the middle of a three-element pattern. Likewise, a question about elements "immediately followed by a number" can never count the final element, here 8.

Some questions first change the series: remove all symbols, drop every vowel, or reverse the second half. Positions are different afterwards, so rewrite the shortened series before counting.

Worked example 6: symbols removed

Without symbols the series becomes R 4 B 7 K M 2 E 9 T 3 P 1 D 5 H 8, which has 17 elements. The 6th from the right is the (17 − 6 + 1) = 12th from the left: P.

When a question reverses part of the series, rewrite only that part and keep the rest in place.

  • Counting in the wrong direction. Convert right-side positions to left-side ones first.
  • Using old positions after a removal. Rewrite the series and recount n.
  • Mixing up "preceded" and "followed". Preceded means the neighbour to the left; followed means the neighbour to the right.
  • Treating a digit as a letter. 0 and O, 1 and I, 5 and S look alike in some fonts; read carefully.
  • Counting the same element twice. Tick each match as you go.

For letter-only series, see letter series.

Q1. In the practice series on this page, which element is 7th from the left? easy

✅ Answer: (b) K

💡 Explanation: Counting from the left: R, 4, #, B, 7, $, K. The 7th element is K.

Q2. In the practice series, how many letters are immediately followed by a number? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 7

💡 Explanation: The pairs are R4, B7, M2, E9, T3, P1 and D5. H is followed by a symbol, so it does not count. That makes 7.

Q3. In the practice series, which element is 4th to the right of the 10th element from the left? medium

✅ Answer: (c) T

💡 Explanation: 4th to the right of position 10 is position 14. The 14th element from the left is T.

Q4. If all symbols are removed from the practice series, which element is 6th from the right? hard

✅ Answer: (a) P

💡 Explanation: Without symbols the series has 17 elements and ends 1 D 5 H 8. Counting back: 8, H, 5, D, 1, P. The 6th from the right is P.

Q5. In the practice series, how many symbols are immediately preceded by a number and immediately followed by a letter? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 6

💡 Explanation: 4#B, 7$K, 2@E, 9%T, 3&P and 1*D fit. The + near the end is preceded by a letter, so it does not count. The answer is 6.

How do I convert a position from the right into one from the left?

In a series of n elements, the k-th element from the right is the (n − k + 1)-th from the left. Count the series once at the start and write n above it.

What does 'immediately preceded by' mean?

The element directly to the left of the one you are looking at. 'Immediately followed by' is the element directly to its right. Read the condition as a little pattern, such as number-symbol-letter, and slide it along the series.

Should I rewrite the series after removing elements?

Yes. Positions change once elements are removed, so write the shortened series out, count its new length, and only then answer position questions.
