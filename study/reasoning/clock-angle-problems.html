Angle Between the Hands of a Clock: The 30H − 5.5M Formula

Hand speeds • 30H − 5.5M • Reflex angles • Hands together • Right angles • Opposite hands

SSC Banking Railway 📅 Last updated: 2026-10-20

Clock questions become arithmetic once you know the speed of each hand in degrees per minute. A full turn is 360 degrees.

  • The minute hand makes a full turn in 60 minutes: 360 ÷ 60 = 6° per minute.
  • The hour hand moves from one number to the next, 30°, in 60 minutes: 0.5° per minute.
  • So the minute hand gains 5.5° per minute on the hour hand.

The most common mistake is forgetting that the hour hand keeps moving between the numbers. At 3:30 the hour hand is halfway between 3 and 4, not on the 3, so the angle is not 90°.

At H hours and M minutes, the hour hand has turned 30H + 0.5M degrees from 12, and the minute hand has turned 6M degrees. The angle between them is the difference:

Angle = |30H − 5.5M|. If the result is more than 180°, subtract it from 360° to get the smaller angle.

Worked example 1: 3:40

|30 × 3 − 5.5 × 40| = |90 − 220| = 130°.

Worked example 2: 7:20

|30 × 7 − 5.5 × 20| = |210 − 110| = 100°. The reflex angle on the other side is 260°.

Worked example 3: 9:45

|30 × 9 − 5.5 × 45| = |270 − 247.5| = 22.5°. The minute hand is on the 9, but the hour hand has moved three-quarters of the way towards 10.

Use H as the hour on a 12-hour dial. At 12 o'clock, H is 0 (or 12, since 360° is the same as 0°).

To find when the hands make an angle θ between H and H + 1 o'clock, solve 30H − 5.5M = ±θ for M. Each sign can give one valid time; keep only the values of M between 0 and 60.

M = (30H ± θ) ÷ 5.5, which is the same as (2/11)(30H ± θ).

Worked example 4: hands together between 4 and 5

Set θ = 0: M = 120 ÷ 5.5 = 21 9/11 ≈ 21.82 minutes past 4.

Worked example 5: right angle between 2 and 3

θ = 90. Taking the minus sign, M = (60 − 90) ÷ 5.5 is negative, so it falls before 2 o'clock and is rejected. Taking the plus sign, M = (60 + 90) ÷ 5.5 = 27 3/11 ≈ 27.27 minutes past 2. A second right angle between 2 and 3 would need M = (60 + 270) ÷ 5.5 = 60, which is 3 o'clock itself.

Worked example 6: opposite between 1 and 2

θ = 180: M = (30 + 180) ÷ 5.5 = 38 2/11 ≈ 38.18 minutes past 1.

Because the minute hand gains 5.5° a minute, it catches up a full 360° every 360 ÷ 5.5 = 720/11 minutes, about 65 minutes 27 seconds. In 12 hours (720 minutes) that happens 11 times.

For a full day, double the 12-hour figure: the hands coincide 24 × 11 ÷ 12 = 22 times.

Right angles occur twice in each gap between meetings, once as the gap widens through 90° and once as it closes through 270°, so there are 2 × 22 = 44 a day.

A related question shows a clock in a mirror and asks for the real time, or the reverse. A vertical mirror swaps left and right, so a hand pointing to 3 appears to point to 9. The real time and the mirror time always add up to 12:00. To find one from the other, subtract from 11:60, which is the same as 12:00 but avoids borrowing.

Worked example 7: the mirror image of 3:40

11:60 − 3:40 = 8:20. In minutes: 720 − 220 = 500 minutes after 12, which is 8 hours 20 minutes. The angle between the hands is the same in both images, 130°.

For a time after 11:00 and before 12:00, subtract from 23:60 instead so that the answer stays on the 12-hour dial.

  • ✘ Keeping the hour hand fixed on the hour number.
  • ✘ Giving the reflex angle when the question wants the smaller one, or the reverse.
  • ✘ Counting 12 or 24 meetings instead of 11 or 22.
  • ✘ Keeping a value of M that is negative or 60 and above when solving for times.

Q1. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 2:30? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 105°

💡 Explanation: |30 × 2 − 5.5 × 30| = |60 − 165| = 105°. The hour hand has moved halfway from 2 to 3, so the answer is not 90°.

Q2. What is the angle between the hands at 5:15? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 67.5°

💡 Explanation: |30 × 5 − 5.5 × 15| = |150 − 82.5| = 67.5°.

Q3. What is the smaller angle between the hands at 11:50? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 55°

💡 Explanation: |30 × 11 − 5.5 × 50| = |330 − 275| = 55°. It is under 180°, so it is already the smaller angle.

Q4. At what time between 6 and 7 o'clock are the hands of a clock together? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 32.73 minutes past 6

💡 Explanation: Set 30 × 6 − 5.5M = 0, so M = 180 ÷ 5.5 = 32 8/11 ≈ 32.73 minutes past 6.

Q5. How many times in a day (24 hours) are the hands of a clock at right angles? medium

✅ Answer: (c) 44

💡 Explanation: The hands are at right angles twice for every time they meet. They meet 22 times a day, so they are at right angles 44 times.

Q6. What is the angle between the hands at 8:20? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 130°

💡 Explanation: |30 × 8 − 5.5 × 20| = |240 − 110| = 130°.

Why does the minute hand gain 5.5 degrees a minute on the hour hand?

The minute hand turns 360 degrees in 60 minutes, which is 6 degrees a minute. The hour hand turns 30 degrees in 60 minutes, which is half a degree a minute. The difference, 5.5 degrees a minute, is how fast the gap between them changes.

Why do the hands meet 11 times in 12 hours, not 12?

The minute hand laps the hour hand once every 720/11 minutes, about 65.45 minutes, not every 60. In 720 minutes that gives 11 meetings. The meeting between 11 and 1 happens exactly at 12 o'clock, so no separate meeting falls between 11 and 12.
