Ganga River System: Source, Prayags, Left and Right Bank Tributaries and Where They Join

Bhagirathi + Alaknanda at Devprayag • 2,525 km • Right bank: Yamuna, Son • Left bank: Ramganga, Gomati, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Mahananda

UPSC SSC Railway State PSC 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01
  • Source: the Ganga rises in the Gangotri glacier near Gaumukh (3,900 m), Uttarkashi district, Uttarakhand, where it is called the Bhagirathi. It cuts through the Central and Lesser Himalayas in narrow gorges.
  • Alaknanda: rises in the Satopanth glacier above Badrinath and joins the Bhagirathi at Devprayag. From there the river is the Ganga.
  • Plains: it enters the plains at Haridwar and flows south, south-east and then east. It splits into two distributaries, the Bhagirathi (Hugli) and the Padma, and reaches the Bay of Bengal near Sagar Island.
  • Size: 2,525 km long, with a basin of about 8.6 lakh sq km in India alone. NCERT calls it the largest river system in India.

The Sarda (Saryu) rises in the Milam glacier in the Nepal Himalayas, where it is called the Goriganga. Along the Indo-Nepal border it is the Kali or Chauk, and it joins the Ghaghara.

Yamuna's own tributaries:

  • Right bank, from the Peninsular plateau: Chambal, Sind, Betwa, Ken.
  • Left bank: Hindan, Rind, Sengar, Varuna.
  • Chambal: rises near Mhow in the Malwa plateau, Madhya Pradesh. It has the Gandhisagar dam above Kota in Rajasthan and flows past Bundi, Sawai Madhopur and Dholpur. It is famous for its badlands, the Chambal ravines.

Q1. At which place do the Bhagirathi and Alaknanda meet to form the Ganga? easy

✅ Answer: (c) Devprayag

💡 Explanation: NCERT says the Bhagirathi meets the Alaknanda at Devprayag, after which the river is called the Ganga. It enters the plains at Haridwar.

Q2. Which of these is a right bank tributary of the Ganga? easy

✅ Answer: (a) Son

💡 Explanation: NCERT names the Son as the Ganga's major right bank tributary. The Gandak, Kosi and Ghaghara join from the left.

Q3. Which river is called the 'sorrow of Bihar'? easy

✅ Answer: (d) Kosi

💡 Explanation: The Kosi brings huge sediment loads and frequently changes its course. The Damodar was once called the sorrow of Bengal.

Q4. Which of these joins the Yamuna on its left bank? medium

✅ Answer: (b) Hindan

💡 Explanation: The Chambal, Sind, Betwa and Ken join the Yamuna from the right, from the Peninsular plateau. The Hindan, Rind, Sengar and Varuna join from the left.

Q5. The Gandak joins the Ganga at: hard

✅ Answer: (c) Sonpur near Patna

💡 Explanation: NCERT says the Gandak joins the Ganga at Sonpur near Patna. The Ghaghara joins at Chhapra, the Ramganga near Kannauj and the Son at Arrah.

Which is the longest tributary of the Ganga?

The Yamuna. NCERT calls it the westernmost and longest tributary. It rises in the Yamunotri glacier on the Banderpunch range and joins the Ganga at Prayag (Allahabad).

Which is the last left bank tributary of the Ganga?

The Mahananda, which rises in the Darjiling hills and joins the Ganga in West Bengal. NCERT calls it the last left bank tributary.

How long is the Ganga and which state has the longest stretch?

NCERT gives 2,525 km. Uttar Pradesh has the longest stretch at 1,450 km, followed by West Bengal (520 km), Bihar (445 km) and Uttarakhand (110 km).
