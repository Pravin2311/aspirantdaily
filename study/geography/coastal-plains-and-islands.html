Coastal Plains and Islands of India: Western vs Eastern Coast, Andaman and Nicobar, and Lakshadweep

West coast: submerged, narrow, ports, kayals, no deltas • East coast: emergent, broad, deltas • Andaman-Nicobar: about 572 islands, Ten Degree Channel • Lakshadweep: coral, 36 islands

UPSC SSC Railway State PSC 📅 Last updated: 2026-11-01

NCERT divides India's coastline by location and the geomorphological processes active on it. The western coastal plain is a submerged coast, and the eastern coastal plain is an emergent coast. That single difference explains most of what exams ask about them: width, deltas and ports.

  • Submergence: the land has sunk, so the sea has drowned the old coast. This leaves a narrow belt, but one with deep, sheltered water that suits natural ports and harbours.
  • Divisions, north to south: the Kachchh and Kathiawar coast in Gujarat, the Konkan coast in Maharashtra, the Goan coast, and the Malabar coast in Karnataka and Kerala.
  • Kayals: the Malabar coast has backwaters called kayals, used for fishing and inland navigation and popular with tourists. The Nehru Trophy Vallamkali boat race is held every year in Punnamada Kayal, Kerala.
  • No deltas: the rivers crossing this plain form no delta. The big west-flowing rivers, the Narmada and the Tapi, run through rift valleys and end in estuaries.
  • Emergence: land has risen relative to the sea, leaving a broad, gently sloping plain.
  • Deltas: the east-flowing rivers, the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri, build well-developed deltas before entering the Bay of Bengal.
  • Ports: because the coast is emergent and the continental shelf runs up to 500 km out, good ports and harbours are harder to develop. There are fewer of them than on the west coast.

The Bay of Bengal group has about 572 islands and islets, between roughly 6°N-14°N and 92°E-94°E. The two principal groups of islets are Ritchie's archipelago and the Labyrinth island.

  • Two parts: the Andaman in the north and the Nicobar in the south, separated by the Ten Degree Channel.
  • Origin: believed to be an elevated portion of submarine mountains. Some smaller islands are volcanic.
  • Barren Island: India's only active volcano.
  • Climate and coasts: convectional rainfall, equatorial vegetation, some coral deposits and beaches.

Q1. Which channel separates the Andaman Islands from the Nicobar Islands? easy

✅ Answer: (b) Ten Degree Channel

💡 Explanation: NCERT says the Andaman in the north and the Nicobar in the south are separated by the Ten Degree Channel. The Nine Degree Channel divides Lakshadweep.

Q2. India's only active volcano, Barren Island, lies in: easy

✅ Answer: (c) The Andaman and Nicobar Islands

💡 Explanation: NCERT says Barren Island, the only active volcano in India, is in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands.

Q3. The western coastal plain is an example of a: medium

✅ Answer: (a) Submerged coast

💡 Explanation: NCERT calls the west coast a submerged coastal plain, which makes it narrow and good for natural ports. The east coast is emergent.

Q4. The largest island of Lakshadweep is: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Minicoy

💡 Explanation: NCERT says Minicoy is the largest island, with an area of 453 sq km. About 11 of the roughly 36 islands are inhabited.

Q5. Why does the east coast have fewer good ports? hard

✅ Answer: (b) It is emergent and its continental shelf extends up to 500 km

💡 Explanation: NCERT links the fewer ports to the emergent nature of the coast and a continental shelf extending up to 500 km into the sea.

What is the difference between the western and eastern coastal plains?

The western plain is narrow and submerged, with natural ports and no deltas. The eastern plain is broad and emergent, with large deltas of the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri but fewer good ports.

How were the Andaman and Nicobar Islands formed?

NCERT says they are believed to be an elevated portion of submarine mountains, with some smaller islands of volcanic origin. Barren Island there is India's only active volcano.

What are the Lakshadweep islands made of?

Coral. NCERT says the whole group is built of coral deposits, has about 36 islands of which 11 are inhabited, and lies 220-440 km off the Kerala coast.
