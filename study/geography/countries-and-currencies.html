Countries and Their Currencies: Region-wise List with ISO Codes, Same-Name Currencies and Recent Changes

"Which country uses the Kyat?" and "What is the currency of Kuwait?" are standard static GK questions in SSC, bank and railway papers. This page lists the currencies of about 80 countries from the official register, ISO 4217, in the version published on 17 September 2026.

Every currency in ISO 4217 has a three-letter alphabetic code and a three-digit numeric code. The Indian Rupee is INR, numeric 356. In most codes the first two letters are the country's own two-letter code and the third is the currency's initial: INR for India's rupee, JPY for Japan's yen, USD for the US dollar. The euro (EUR) is the main exception because it belongs to no single country.

Gulf currencies come in three families: riyal or rial (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Iran), dinar (Kuwait, Bahrain, Iraq, Jordan) and dirham (UAE). Questions often hide one odd member in a list of four.

The euro (EUR) is listed for every euro-area country. Among those exams ask about most: France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, the Netherlands, Croatia and Bulgaria. The 2026 list shows the euro for Bulgaria, so check older notes against it.

Oceania is quick: Australia uses the Australian Dollar (AUD) and New Zealand the New Zealand Dollar (NZD).

  • Rupee (6): India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Mauritius and Seychelles. Not Indonesia (Rupiah) or the Maldives (Rufiyaa).
  • Dinar (5 in this list): Kuwait, Bahrain, Iraq, Jordan and Algeria.
  • Riyal or rial: Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman and Iran.
  • Dirham: UAE and Morocco.
  • Pound: United Kingdom (Pound Sterling), Egypt and South Sudan.
  • Peso: Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Cuba and the Philippines.
  • Shilling: Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.
  • Won: South Korea and North Korea, with different codes (KRW and KPW).
  • Bulgaria and Croatia now appear with the euro.
  • Zimbabwe is listed with Zimbabwe Gold (ZWG).
  • Sierra Leone's Leone carries the code SLE.
  • Venezuela has two Bolívar Soberano codes, VES and VED.

Older guidebooks still print the previous currencies, so always prefer the latest list for these four.

  • ✘ Rupiah (Indonesia) and Rufiyaa (Maldives) are not rupees.
  • ✘ Krona is Swedish; krone is Norwegian and Danish.
  • ✘ Qatar and Oman use rials, not dinars; the UAE uses the dirham.
  • ✘ The UK did not adopt the euro; its currency is the Pound Sterling.
  • ✘ Bulgaria is now listed with the euro; older books may show a different currency.

Q1. What is the currency of Bangladesh? easy

✅ Answer: (a) Taka

💡 Explanation: ISO 4217 lists the Taka (BDT) for Bangladesh. The Kyat is Myanmar's currency and the Rufiyaa is the Maldives'.

Q2. Which country's currency is the Ngultrum? easy

✅ Answer: (b) Bhutan

💡 Explanation: The Ngultrum (BTN) is Bhutan's currency; the ISO list also records the Indian Rupee as a currency used in Bhutan.

Q3. The Rupiah is the currency of: easy

✅ Answer: (c) Indonesia

💡 Explanation: Indonesia uses the Rupiah (IDR). Mauritius and Sri Lanka use rupees, and the Maldives uses the Rufiyaa.

Q4. Which of these countries does NOT use a dinar? medium

✅ Answer: (d) Qatar

💡 Explanation: Qatar uses the Qatari Rial (QAR). Kuwait, Bahrain and Jordan all use dinars.

Q5. Which of these countries uses the euro as per the 2026 ISO 4217 list? medium

✅ Answer: (a) Bulgaria

💡 Explanation: The September 2026 list shows the Euro for Bulgaria. Poland uses the Zloty, Hungary the Forint and Sweden the Krona.

Q6. Which currency is listed for Zimbabwe in ISO 4217? hard

✅ Answer: (b) Zimbabwe Gold (ZWG)

💡 Explanation: The current ISO 4217 list gives Zimbabwe Gold, code ZWG, for Zimbabwe.

Q7. Which two countries use the US Dollar, according to the ISO list? medium

✅ Answer: (c) Ecuador and El Salvador

💡 Explanation: ISO 4217 lists the US Dollar for Ecuador, and both the US Dollar and the El Salvador Colon for El Salvador.

Q8. The Hryvnia is the currency of: medium

✅ Answer: (d) Ukraine

💡 Explanation: Ukraine's currency is the Hryvnia (UAH). Russia uses the Russian Ruble, Czechia the Czech Koruna and Kazakhstan the Tenge.

Which countries have a currency called the rupee?

India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Mauritius and Seychelles all have rupees in the ISO 4217 list. Indonesia's Rupiah and the Maldives' Rufiyaa are separate currencies with similar names.

What is the ISO code for the Indian Rupee?

The Indian Rupee's alphabetic code is INR and its numeric code is 356, as listed in ISO 4217. Bhutan also appears in the list with the Indian Rupee alongside its own Ngultrum.

Which countries use the euro?

The euro is used by many EU members, including France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, the Netherlands, Croatia and Bulgaria. Poland, Hungary, Sweden, Denmark and Czechia keep their own currencies.
