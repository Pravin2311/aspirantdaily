Rhombus: area from diagonals

Diagonals 16 cm and 12 cm. Area = 16 × 12 ÷ 2 = 96 cm². Half-diagonals 8 and 6 make a right triangle, so each side is 10 cm.

NCERT defines a sector as the region between two radii and their arc, and a segment as the region between a chord and its arc. Angles θ are in degrees.

Circle with π = 22/7

Radius 7 cm. Area = 22/7 × 49 = 154 cm² and circumference = 44 cm.

Sector

Radius 14 cm, angle 45°. Area = (45/360) × 22/7 × 196 = 77 cm², and the arc is (45/360) × 88 = 11 cm.

Segment of a right-angled sector

Radius 10 cm, angle 90°. Sector = π × 100/4 ≈ 78.54 cm², triangle = ½ × 10 × 10 = 50 cm², so the segment is about 28.54 cm².

Ring (circular path)

A path lies between circles of radius 10 m and 6 m. Area = π(100 − 36) = 64π ≈ 201.06 m².

Common traps: leaving out the diameter in a semicircle's perimeter; using diameter for radius; mixing cm and m before multiplying; and using the diagonal formula for a rectangle, which is not a rhombus. For solids, see cube and cuboid and cylinder, cone and sphere.

Q1. The diagonal of a square is 12 cm. What is its area? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 72 cm²

💡 Explanation: Area of a square = diagonal² ÷ 2 = 144 ÷ 2 = 72 cm². 144 would be the area if 12 cm were the side.

Q2. A wheel of radius 35 cm rolls along a road. How many full turns does it make in 1.1 km? (Take π = 22/7.) medium

✅ Answer: (c) 500

💡 Explanation: Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 35 = 220 cm. 1.1 km = 1,10,000 cm, so the wheel turns 1,10,000 ÷ 220 = 500 times.

Q3. A rectangular plot has perimeter 46 m and length 15 m. What is its area? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 120 m²

💡 Explanation: Length + breadth = 46 ÷ 2 = 23, so breadth = 8 m and area = 15 × 8 = 120 m².

Q4. An equilateral triangle has area 36√3 cm². What is its perimeter? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 36 cm

💡 Explanation: (√3/4)a² = 36√3 gives a² = 144, so a = 12 cm and the perimeter is 3 × 12 = 36 cm.

Q5. What is the perimeter of a semicircle of radius 7 cm? (Take π = 22/7.) easy

✅ Answer: (c) 36 cm

💡 Explanation: A semicircle's boundary is the curved half (πr = 22 cm) plus the diameter (14 cm), giving 36 cm. Forgetting the diameter gives 22.

Should I use π = 22/7 or 3.14?

Use whatever the question says. If it is silent, 22/7 is usually intended when the radius is a multiple of 7, and 3.14 otherwise. Check the options: they are normally written to suit one choice.

How do I remember which formula uses the diagonals?

The rhombus and the square (a special rhombus) have area = half the product of the diagonals, because the diagonals cross at right angles. A general rectangle or parallelogram does not, because its diagonals are not perpendicular.

When do I need Heron's formula?

When all three sides of a triangle are known but the height is not. Find s = (a + b + c)/2, then area = √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)).
