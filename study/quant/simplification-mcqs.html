Simplification is mostly about doing operations in the right order. BODMAS sets that order:

  1. Brackets: innermost first, usually ( ), then { }, then [ ].
  2. Orders: powers and roots.
  3. Division and Multiplication: equal priority, worked left to right.
  4. Addition and Subtraction: equal priority, worked left to right.

Most mistakes come from step 3. In 48 ÷ 6 × 2, division and multiplication have the same rank, so you work left to right: 48 ÷ 6 = 8, then 8 × 2 = 16. Doing 6 × 2 first gives a wrong answer. Q1. 48 ÷ 6 × 2 + 5 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (d) 21

💡 Explanation: Division and multiplication have equal priority, so go left to right: 48 ÷ 6 = 8, then 8 × 2 = 16, then 16 + 5 = 21. Doing 6 × 2 first gives 9, the classic trap.

Q2. 120 − 15 × 4 + 18 ÷ 3 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 66

💡 Explanation: Multiply and divide before adding and subtracting: 15 × 4 = 60 and 18 ÷ 3 = 6, so 120 − 60 + 6 = 66.

Q3. 7 + 3 × (12 − 4)² ÷ 16 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 19

💡 Explanation: Bracket first: 12 − 4 = 8. Power next: 8² = 64. Then 3 × 64 ÷ 16 = 12, and 7 + 12 = 19.

Q4. [36 ÷ (4 + 2)] × [5 − (8 − 6)] = ? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 18

💡 Explanation: Solve each bracket from the inside: 36 ÷ 6 = 6 and 5 − 2 = 3. Then 6 × 3 = 18.

Q5. 5 − [4 − {3 − (2 − 1)}] = ? medium

✅ Answer: (a) 3

💡 Explanation: Open the innermost bracket first: 2 − 1 = 1, then 3 − 1 = 2, then 4 − 2 = 2, and finally 5 − 2 = 3.

Q6. 0.25 × 0.4 + 1.5 ÷ 0.3 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (d) 5.1

💡 Explanation: 0.25 × 0.4 = 0.1. For 1.5 ÷ 0.3, shift both decimals one place: 15 ÷ 3 = 5. So 0.1 + 5 = 5.1.

Q7. 0.0072 ÷ 0.012 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (c) 0.6

💡 Explanation: Multiply both numbers by 10,000 to clear decimals: 72 ÷ 120 = 0.6. Count the decimal shifts on both sides equally.

Q8. (3.6 × 0.48 × 2.5) ÷ (0.12 × 0.09 × 0.5) = ? hard

✅ Answer: (c) 800

💡 Explanation: Cancel in pairs: 3.6 ÷ 0.09 = 40, 0.48 ÷ 0.12 = 4 and 2.5 ÷ 0.5 = 5. Then 40 × 4 × 5 = 800.

Q9. 3/4 + 5/6 − 7/12 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 1

💡 Explanation: Use the LCM 12: 9/12 + 10/12 − 7/12 = 12/12 = 1.

Q10. 2/3 of 3/4 of 480 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 240

💡 Explanation: 'Of' means multiply: 3/4 of 480 = 360, and 2/3 of 360 = 240. Or note 2/3 × 3/4 = 1/2, so the answer is half of 480.

Q11. 3½ + 2¾ − 1¼ = ? easy

✅ Answer: (d) 5

💡 Explanation: Add whole parts and fraction parts separately: 3 + 2 − 1 = 4 and ½ + ¾ − ¼ = 1. Total 4 + 1 = 5.

Q12. 1/2 + 1/6 + 1/12 + 1/20 = ? hard

✅ Answer: (d) 0.8 (= 4/5)

💡 Explanation: Each term is 1/(n(n+1)) = 1/n − 1/(n+1), so the sum telescopes to 1 − 1/5 = 4/5 = 0.8.

Q13. 1 + 1/(1 + 1/(1 + 1/2)) = ? hard

✅ Answer: (c) 1.6 (= 8/5)

💡 Explanation: Work from the bottom: 1 + 1/2 = 3/2, its reciprocal is 2/3, 1 + 2/3 = 5/3, its reciprocal is 3/5, and 1 + 3/5 = 8/5 = 1.6.

Q14. 25% of 480 + 40% of 350 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 260

💡 Explanation: 25% is a quarter: 480 ÷ 4 = 120. 40% of 350 = 4 × 35 = 140. Total 120 + 140 = 260.

Q15. 18% of 250 − 12% of 150 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 27

💡 Explanation: 18% of 250 = 45 and 12% of 150 = 18, so 45 − 18 = 27. Do not subtract the percentages first.

Q16. 15% of 15% of 4000 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (a) 90

💡 Explanation: 15% of 4000 = 600, and 15% of 600 = 90. Successive percentages multiply; they do not add to 30%.

Q17. 12.5% of 640 + 37.5% of 320 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 200

💡 Explanation: 12.5% = 1/8 and 37.5% = 3/8: 640 ÷ 8 = 80 and 3 × 40 = 120. Total 80 + 120 = 200.

Q18. √1764 + √225 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (c) 57

💡 Explanation: 1764 ends in 4 and lies between 40² = 1600 and 45² = 2025; 42² = 1764. With √225 = 15, the sum is 42 + 15 = 57.

Q19. ∛2744 − ∛343 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 7

💡 Explanation: 2744 ends in 4, and only cubes of numbers ending in 4 end in 4; 14³ = 2744. With ∛343 = 7, the difference is 14 − 7 = 7.

Q20. √0.0169 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 0.13

💡 Explanation: 0.0169 = 169 ÷ 10,000, so its root is 13 ÷ 100 = 0.13. A square root halves the number of decimal places: four become two.

Q21. 2⁵ + 3³ − 4² = ? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 43

💡 Explanation: 2⁵ = 32, 3³ = 27 and 4² = 16, so 32 + 27 − 16 = 43.

Q22. 64^(2/3) + 81^(1/4) = ? hard

✅ Answer: (d) 19

💡 Explanation: 64^(1/3) = 4, so 64^(2/3) = 4² = 16. 81^(1/4) = 3 because 3⁴ = 81. Total 16 + 3 = 19.

Q23. 35² − 25² = ? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 600

💡 Explanation: Use a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b): (35 + 25)(35 − 25) = 60 × 10 = 600. Squaring 35 − 25 = 10 gives the wrong 100.

Q24. 99 × 101 = ? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 9999

💡 Explanation: Write it as (100 − 1)(100 + 1) = 100² − 1² = 10,000 − 1 = 9,999.

Q25. 998 × 1002 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 999996

💡 Explanation: (1000 − 2)(1000 + 2) = 1000² − 2² = 10,00,000 − 4 = 9,99,996.

Q26. 1.2 × 1.2 + 2 × 1.2 × 0.8 + 0.8 × 0.8 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (a) 4

💡 Explanation: This is a² + 2ab + b² with a = 1.2 and b = 0.8, so it equals (1.2 + 0.8)² = 2² = 4.

Q27. 45 × 45 + 2 × 45 × 55 + 55 × 55 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 10000

💡 Explanation: Again (a + b)² with a = 45 and b = 55: (45 + 55)² = 100² = 10,000. Spot the pattern instead of multiplying.

Q28. (7.5² − 2.5²) ÷ (7.5 − 2.5) = ? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 10

💡 Explanation: (a² − b²) ÷ (a − b) = a + b, so the answer is 7.5 + 2.5 = 10, with no squaring needed.

Q29. (0.5 + 0.25) ÷ (0.5 − 0.25) = ? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 3

💡 Explanation: 0.75 ÷ 0.25 = 3. Simplify each bracket before dividing; dividing term by term is not allowed.

Q30. 666 ÷ 18 × 3 ÷ 37 = ? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 3

💡 Explanation: Left to right: 666 ÷ 18 = 37, 37 × 3 = 111, 111 ÷ 37 = 3. Treating 18 × 3 as one divisor gives the wrong 1/3.

What is the order of operations in simplification?

Brackets first (innermost outwards), then powers and roots, then division and multiplication from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right. This is what BODMAS stands for.

How can I simplify faster in the exam?

Look for patterns before calculating: a² − b², (a + b)², fractions that cancel, and percentages that are simple fractions such as 12.5% = 1/8. Recognising a pattern often replaces several multiplications.
