A company has 800 employees, of whom 40% are women. A quarter of the women and half of the men work in the IT department; everyone else works outside IT.

Q1. Set 1 (table). By what percentage did sales of children's books rise from 2024 to 2025? easy

✅ Answer: (b) 30%

💡 Explanation: Children's books rose from 90 to 117 hundred copies. The increase is 27 on a base of 90: 27 ÷ 90 × 100 = 30%. Dividing by the new value, 117, is the usual mistake.

Q2. Set 1 (table). What were the shop's total book sales in 2025, in hundred copies? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 413

💡 Explanation: Add the 2025 column: 150 + 92 + 54 + 117 = 413 hundred copies.

Q3. Set 1 (table). By what percentage did history sales fall from 2024 to 2025? medium

✅ Answer: (a) 10%

💡 Explanation: History fell from 60 to 54, a drop of 6 on a base of 60. 6 ÷ 60 × 100 = 10%. The 11.1% option divides by 54 instead of the starting value.

Q4. Set 2 (bar graph). What is the average number of students who passed per school? easy

✅ Answer: (d) 140

💡 Explanation: The five bars add to 120 + 150 + 90 + 180 + 160 = 700 students. 700 ÷ 5 = 140.

Q5. Set 2 (bar graph). The number who passed in school D is what percentage more than in school C? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 100%

💡 Explanation: D has 180 and C has 90. The difference, 90, equals C's whole value, so D is 100% more. 200% is D as a percentage of C, a different question.

Q6. Set 2 (bar graph). What is the ratio of students who passed in school B to those in school E? easy

✅ Answer: (c) 15 : 16

💡 Explanation: B has 150 and E has 160. Dividing both by 10 gives 15 : 16, which has no further common factor.

Q7. Set 3 (pie chart). How much does the hostel spend on staff each month? easy

✅ Answer: (a) ₹30,000

💡 Explanation: Staff takes 25% of ₹1,20,000. A quarter of 1,20,000 is ₹30,000.

Q8. Set 3 (pie chart). What is the central angle of the electricity sector? medium

✅ Answer: (c) 54°

💡 Explanation: Each 1% of a pie chart is 3.6°, so 15% is 15 × 3.6 = 54°.

Q9. Set 3 (pie chart). By how much does monthly spending on food exceed spending on maintenance? medium

✅ Answer: (b) ₹33,600

💡 Explanation: Food is 40% and maintenance 12%, a gap of 28 percentage points. 28% of ₹1,20,000 = ₹33,600. Subtracting the percentages first saves one multiplication.

Q10. Set 4 (line graph). In which year did profit grow by the highest percentage over the previous year? medium

✅ Answer: (d) 2025

💡 Explanation: Growth was 25% in 2022 (20 to 25), 20% in 2023 (25 to 30), −20% in 2024 (30 to 24) and 50% in 2025 (24 to 36). The 2025 rise has both the largest gain and a small base.

Q11. Set 4 (line graph). What was the firm's average annual profit over the five years? easy

✅ Answer: (d) ₹27 lakh

💡 Explanation: The five values add to 20 + 25 + 30 + 24 + 36 = 135 lakh. 135 ÷ 5 = ₹27 lakh.

Q12. Set 4 (line graph). By what percentage did profit fall in 2024? easy

✅ Answer: (a) 20%

💡 Explanation: Profit fell from 30 to 24 lakh, a drop of 6 on a base of 30. 6 ÷ 30 × 100 = 20%. Using 24 as the base gives the wrong answer of 25%.

Q13. Set 5 (caselet). How many employees work in IT? medium

✅ Answer: (b) 320

💡 Explanation: Women: 40% of 800 = 320, and 25% of them, 80, are in IT. Men: 480, and half of them, 240, are in IT. Total IT = 80 + 240 = 320.

Q14. Set 5 (caselet). What percentage of the IT staff are women? medium

✅ Answer: (c) 25%

💡 Explanation: 80 of the 320 IT employees are women. 80 ÷ 320 × 100 = 25%. The 40% option is the share of women in the whole company, not in IT.

Q15. Set 5 (caselet). What is the ratio of men outside IT to women outside IT? hard

✅ Answer: (d) 1 : 1

💡 Explanation: Men outside IT: 480 − 240 = 240. Women outside IT: 320 − 80 = 240. The ratio is 240 : 240 = 1 : 1.

How many DI sets should I practise each day?

Quality matters more than volume. Two or three timed sets a day, each reviewed afterwards for why any answer went wrong, builds speed faster than racing through ten. Keep a note of the trap that caught you and look for it next time.

How much time should one DI set take in a bank exam?

There is no fixed rule, but most candidates aim for roughly a minute per question in prelims, slightly more in mains. Reading the chart carefully once at the start is what makes the later questions quick.
