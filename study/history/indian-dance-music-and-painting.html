🎭 Indian Dance, Music & Painting

Classical Dances • Folk Dances • Hindustani & Carnatic • Miniature Painting • Folk Painting • UNESCO Heritage

High Yield History 📅 Last updated: Oct 2026
🔍 Quick Search: 8 classical dances of India and states, Sattriya added year, Carnatic trinity names, dhrupad khayal thumri, Basohli Kangra painting, Madhubani Warli Pattachitra state, Garba UNESCO
  • The Sangeet Natak Akademi recognises eight classical dances; the Ministry of Culture also includes Chhau, making nine. Exams most often ask you to match a dance to its state.
  • All classical dances trace their theory to the Natya Shastra of Bharata Muni, which describes rasa (aesthetic emotion) and bhava (expression).
  • Indian music divided into two streams — Hindustani in the north and Carnatic in the south — from around the 13th–14th centuries.

Hindustani and Carnatic Music

HindustaniCarnatic
RegionNorth IndiaSouth India
InfluencesPersian and Central Asian, through court patronageLargely indigenous and devotional
Scope for improvisationGreater; organised in gharanasMore fixed compositions (kritis)
Main instrumentsSitar, sarod, tabla, sarangi, shehnaiVeena, mridangam, violin, ghatam, nadaswaram
Key namesAmir Khusrau (credited with qawwali and tarana), Tansen (Akbar's court)Purandara Dasa ("father of Carnatic music"); the Trinity — Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri

Hindustani vocal forms: Dhrupad (the oldest, austere and devotional; the Dagar family is its best-known lineage), Khayal (the most popular, more ornamented), Thumri (romantic, light classical) and Tappa (quick, from Punjabi camel-drivers' songs). Both systems are built on raga (melodic framework) and tala (rhythmic cycle).

Schools of Painting

  • Ajanta murals — the finest early paintings, using a fresco-like technique on plaster.
  • Pala school (Bengal, Bihar, 8th–12th century) — palm-leaf Buddhist manuscripts; the earliest Indian miniatures.
  • Mughal school — begun when Humayun brought the Persian masters Mir Sayyid Ali and Abdus Samad. Under Akbar, the workshop produced the illustrated Hamzanama. It peaked under Jahangir, whose artists — Ustad Mansur (birds and animals) and Abul Hasan — excelled at naturalistic portraits.
  • Rajasthani schools — Mewar, Bundi, Kota and Kishangarh, famous for Bani Thani by Nihal Chand, sometimes called the "Indian Mona Lisa".
  • Pahari schools — Basohli (bold colours, J&K) and Kangra (lyrical, Himachal Pradesh), often showing Radha and Krishna.
  • Company painting — Indian artists working for British patrons in the 18th–19th centuries; Raja Ravi Varma later combined European realism with Indian themes.

India on UNESCO's Intangible Heritage List

Examples include Vedic chanting, Ramlila, Kutiyattam (Sanskrit theatre of Kerala), Ramman (Garhwal), Mudiyettu (Kerala), Kalbelia and Chhau (2010), Manipuri Sankirtana, Yoga (2016), Kumbh Mela (2017), Durga Puja in Kolkata (2021) and Garba of Gujarat (2023).

Q1. Sattriya, recognised as a classical dance in 2000, belongs to:

✅ Answer: (c) Assam

💡 Explanation: Sattriya was created by Srimanta Sankardeva in the Vaishnava monasteries (sattras) of Assam.

Q2. Which of the following is NOT one of the Carnatic music Trinity?

✅ Answer: (b) Purandara Dasa

💡 Explanation: Purandara Dasa is called the father of Carnatic music but predates the Trinity.

Q3. Warli painting is a tribal art form of:

✅ Answer: (a) Maharashtra

💡 Explanation: Warli paintings are made by the Warli tribe of Maharashtra using white figures on mud walls.

Q4. Mughal painting reached its peak, especially in naturalistic studies of birds and animals, under:

✅ Answer: (d) Jahangir

💡 Explanation: Jahangir's court artists such as Ustad Mansur excelled in nature studies.

Q5. The oldest surviving form of Hindustani classical vocal music is:

✅ Answer: (c) Dhrupad

💡 Explanation: Dhrupad is the oldest and most austere form.

How many classical dances are there in India?

The Sangeet Natak Akademi recognises eight: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Kathakali, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam and Sattriya. The Ministry of Culture's list also includes Chhau, making nine.

What is the difference between Hindustani and Carnatic music?

Hindustani music, from North India, absorbed Persian influences through court patronage and allows more improvisation within gharanas. Carnatic music, from South India, is largely devotional and built on fixed compositions called kritis. Both are based on raga and tala.
