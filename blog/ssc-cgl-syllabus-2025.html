SSC CGL 2026 Syllabus & Exam Pattern (Official Notice)

Published December 15, 2025 | Updated October 20, 2026 against SSC's CGL 2026 notice of 21.05.2026

This guide summarises the scheme of examination and indicative syllabus in the Staff Selection Commission's Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 notice (uploaded 21.05.2026, paras 13.8–13.11 and 16). Where we add our own advice, we say so.

CGL 2026 schedule: applications closed on 22.06.2026. SSC's notice of 12.09.2026 fixed Tier-I (CBE) for 30 September to 30 October 2026. The main notice gives Tier-II as December 2026 (tentative). Check ssc.gov.in for changes.

📌 Exam pattern overview

CGL 2026 has two computer-based tiers. The notice has no interview stage. Tier-I marks are used only to shortlist candidates for Tier-II; the final merit list is prepared on Tier-II performance only (para 16.10).

📚 Indicative syllabus (Tier-I), summarised from para 13.10

1. General Intelligence & Reasoning

Verbal and non-verbal questions: analogies (semantic, number, figural), classification, series, coding-decoding, Venn diagrams, syllogistic reasoning, statement and conclusion, space visualisation and orientation, paper folding and punched holes, embedded figures, indexing and address/date matching, and critical, emotional and social intelligence.

2. General Awareness

Current events and everyday science, plus India and its neighbouring countries: history, culture, geography, economic scene, general policy and scientific research. Our Study Portal covers these subject by subject.

3. Quantitative Aptitude (10th-standard level)

Whole numbers, decimals and fractions; percentage, ratio and proportion, square roots, averages, interest, profit and loss, discount, partnership, mixture and alligation, time and distance, time and work; school algebra and elementary surds, graphs of linear equations; triangles, circles, polygons and mensuration of standard solids; trigonometric ratios, identities, heights and distances; histograms, frequency polygons, bar and pie charts.

4. English Comprehension

The notice describes this only as the ability to understand correct English, basic comprehension and writing ability. Tier-II English (para 13.11.3) lists the detailed areas: vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, error spotting, fill-in-the-blanks, idioms, one-word substitution, voice, narration, cloze passages and comprehension.

🎯 Which papers do you need?

  • Most posts (Assistant Section Officer, Inspector, Auditor, Tax Assistant, etc.): Tier-I, then Tier-II Paper-I.
  • Junior Statistical Officer / Statistical Investigator Grade-II: Paper-I + Paper-II (Statistics).
  • Assistant Audit Officer / Assistant Accounts Officer: Paper-I + Paper-III.
  • Posts with physical standards (for example Inspector posts in CBIC, Inspector and Sub-Inspector in CBN, Sub-Inspector in CBI and NIA): physical and medical standards are checked by the user department after final selection.

🎯 Preparation tips (our advice, not from the notice)

  • Practise under sectional timers: from CGL 2026 each Tier-I section has its own 15 minutes, so you cannot carry time over from one section to another.
  • Treat Tier-II accuracy seriously: a wrong answer in Paper-I costs a full mark, a third of what a correct one earns.
  • Do not neglect the qualifying sections: failing the Computer Knowledge Test or DEST removes you regardless of your other scores.
  • English carries the most Tier-II marks: 45 of the 130 questions in Sections I and II.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are SSC CGL Tier-I marks counted in the final merit?

No. Under the 2026 notice, Tier-I marks are used to shortlist candidates for Tier-II, and the final merit list is based on Tier-II performance only.

What is the negative marking in SSC CGL?

Tier-I: −0.50 per wrong answer (each question carries 2 marks). Tier-II: −1 per wrong answer in Sections I–III of Paper-I (questions carry 3 marks), and −0.50 in Paper-II and Paper-III.

Who needs to write SSC CGL Tier-II Paper-II?

Only candidates shortlisted for Junior Statistical Officer and Statistical Investigator Grade-II posts.
