SSC CGL Syllabus 2025 & Exam Pattern

Last updated: December 15, 2025 | Official SSC CGL Tier 1 & Tier 2 Syllabus

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Combined Graduate Level (CGL) 2025 exam is one of India’s most sought-after government recruitment exams. This guide covers the complete SSC CGL 2025 syllabus and exam pattern for both Tier 1 and Tier 2, based on the latest official notification.

📌 SSC CGL 2025 Exam Pattern Overview

The exam is conducted in two main tiers (Tier 1 and Tier 2), followed by document verification. There is no interview for any post.

Tier 1: Computer-Based Objective Test

Section Questions Marks Duration

(80 mins for PwBD)

Total: 100 questions | 200 marks | −0.50 for each wrong answer. Tier 1 is a screening test: it decides who reaches Tier 2 but its marks are not added to the final merit.

Tier 2: Computer-Based Objective Tests

Tier 2 has a compulsory Paper I for all posts, plus Paper II or Paper III only for certain posts:

📚 Detailed SSC CGL 2025 Syllabus

1. General Intelligence & Reasoning (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Paper I)

  • Analogies, Similarities and Differences
  • Space Visualization, Problem Solving
  • Analysis, Judgment, Decision Making
  • Visual Memory, Discrimination, Observation
  • Arithmetical Reasoning, Verbal & Figure Classification
  • Venn Diagrams, Syllogism, Coding-Decoding

2. General Awareness (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Paper I)

  • Current Affairs (Last 6–12 months)
  • History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern India)
  • Geography (India & World)
  • Polity (Indian Constitution, Governance)
  • Economy (Budget, Schemes, Indices)
  • Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Static GK (Books, Awards, Sports, Important Days)

3. Quantitative Aptitude (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Paper I)

  • Number Systems, HCF & LCM
  • Percentages, Profit & Loss, Discount
  • Ratio & Proportion, Partnership
  • Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns
  • Speed, Time & Distance
  • Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration
  • Data Interpretation (Tables, Graphs, Charts)

4. English Comprehension (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Paper I)

  • Reading Comprehension
  • Grammar (Tenses, Voice, Narration)
  • Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms)
  • Sentence Correction & Improvement
  • Fill in the Blanks, Error Detection
  • One-word Substitution, Spelling

🎯 Preparation Tips for SSC CGL 2025

  • Focus on high-weightage topics: DI, Algebra, Current Affairs, Grammar
  • Practice daily: Solve at least 25 MCQs per subject
  • Take full-length mocks: Simulate real exam conditions
  • Revise weekly: Use notes for GK and formulas

🎯 Which Paper Do You Need?

  • Most posts (Assistant Section Officer, Inspector, Auditor, Tax Assistant, etc.): Tier 1 + Tier 2 Paper I.
  • Junior Statistical Officer: Paper I + Paper II (Statistics).
  • Assistant Audit / Accounts Officer: Paper I + Paper III, in years when SSC advertises these posts.
  • Posts with physical standards (e.g., Inspector in CBN or CBIC) also require physical tests.

📊 Topic Weightage to Plan Around

Tier 1 tests breadth, Tier 2 depth. In Tier 2, English (45 questions) is the biggest scoring block, so grammar and reading speed pay off twice. Advanced maths — algebra, geometry, trigonometry and mensuration — carries more weight in Tier 2 than in Tier 1. General Awareness is the same syllabus in both tiers, so one set of static GK notes serves both.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are SSC CGL Tier 1 marks counted in the final merit?

No. Tier 1 is a screening test. Its marks decide who is shortlisted for Tier 2, and final merit is based on Tier 2.

What is the negative marking in SSC CGL?

In Tier 1, 0.50 marks are deducted for each wrong answer (each question carries 2 marks). In Tier 2 Paper I, questions carry 3 marks and wrong answers are also penalised; check the notification for the exact deduction.

Who needs to write SSC CGL Tier 2 Paper II?

Only candidates applying for Junior Statistical Officer and Statistical Investigator Grade II posts, who must take the Statistics paper.
