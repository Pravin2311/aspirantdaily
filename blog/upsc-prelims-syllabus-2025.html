UPSC Prelims Syllabus & Exam Pattern (CSE 2026 Notice)

Published December 18, 2025 | Updated October 20, 2026 against UPSC Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE

The Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination is the screening stage for the IAS, IPS, IFS and other civil services. This page reproduces the official Prelims syllabus and rules from UPSC's Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE (dated 04.02.2026), then maps each topic to our study notes.

Where the cycle stands: under that notice, CSE 2026 Prelims were held on 24 May 2026. The CSE 2027 notice had not been checked for this page. The Prelims syllabus below is the 2026 text; confirm it against the 2027 notice on upsc.gov.in when it is published.

📌 Exam pattern (from the 2026 notice)

The Prelims has two compulsory objective papers of 200 marks each, each of two hours, set in Hindi and English. General Studies Paper-II (CSAT) is qualifying with a minimum of 33%. Candidates are shortlisted for the Mains on the Paper-I marks, among those who clear the Paper-II minimum.

Paper Marks Duration Role
General Studies Paper-I 200 2 hours Decides who is shortlisted for the Mains
General Studies Paper-II (CSAT) 200 2 hours Qualifying: minimum 33%

Negative marking applies to every question in both papers. Each question has four options. A wrong answer, or more than one answer, costs one-third (0.33) of the marks for that question. A question left blank carries no penalty. The notice does not fix the number of questions in each paper; check the latest question papers on upsc.gov.in.

📚 Official syllabus: General Studies Paper-I

This is the complete official text (Appendix I, Section III, Part A of the notice):

  • Current events of national and international importance.
  • History of India and Indian National Movement.
  • Indian and World Geography – Physical, Social, Economic Geography of India and the World.
  • Indian Polity and Governance – Constitution, Political System, Panchayati Raj, Public Policy, Rights Issues, etc.
  • Economic and Social Development – Sustainable Development, Poverty, Inclusion, Demographics, Social Sector Initiatives, etc.
  • General issues on Environmental ecology, Bio-diversity and Climate Change – that do not require subject specialization.
  • General Science.

🧠 Official syllabus: General Studies Paper-II (CSAT)

  • Comprehension;
  • Interpersonal skills including communication skills;
  • Logical reasoning and analytical ability;
  • Decision making and problem solving;
  • General mental ability;
  • Basic numeracy (numbers and their relations, orders of magnitude, etc.) (Class X level), Data interpretation (charts, graphs, tables, data sufficiency etc. – Class X level).

🗺️ How each syllabus line maps to our study notes

The official syllabus is deliberately broad. The breakdown below is AspirantDaily's own study plan, not UPSC text: it shows where each line is covered in our notes.

Official syllabus line What to study Our notes
Current events Government schemes, policies, bills, summits and science news of roughly the past year Current Affairs, Schemes, International Relations

Indian and World Geography Geomorphology, climate, oceans, Indian physiography, rivers, soils, economic geography Geography
Indian Polity and Governance Constitution, Parliament, executive, judiciary, federalism, local government, constitutional bodies Polity
Economic and Social Development National income, inflation, money and banking, budget, taxation, external sector, social sector schemes Economy

General Science Everyday physics, chemistry and biology, plus space, defence and emerging technology Science & Tech
