Current Affairs Summary – December 2025

Published on December 22, 2025 | For UPSC, SSC, Bank, RRB & PSC Exams

Here’s a concise summary of the most important current affairs from December 1 to December 21, 2025, curated specifically for Indian competitive exam aspirants. This includes national developments, key appointments, international events involving India, economic updates, and recent cultural recognitions.

🇮🇳 National Highlights

  • GDP Growth Q2 FY26: India’s real GDP grew 8.2% in July–Sept 2025 (Q2 FY26) — a six-quarter high — as per NSO data released on November 28, 2025.
  • Chief of Army Staff: General Upendra Dwivedi, the 30th COAS, has led the Army since June 30, 2024, succeeding General Manoj Pande.
  • DPDP Rules, 2025: Notified on November 13, 2025, operationalising the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Provisions establishing the Data Protection Board took effect immediately; most compliance obligations apply after 18 months.

🌍 International & Bilateral

  • 23rd India–Russia Annual Summit: President Vladimir Putin made a state visit to New Delhi on December 4–5, 2025 — his first since December 2021.
  • India–EU FTA Talks: Negotiations resumed in New Delhi on December 8, 2025, following the 14th round in Brussels (October 6–10, 2025), with both sides aiming to conclude the deal. (Update: the FTA was concluded at the 16th India–EU Summit on January 27, 2026.)
  • COP30: The UN Climate Change Conference was held in Belém, Brazil, from November 10–21, 2025.

💼 Economy & Policy

  • RBI Monetary Policy: On December 5, 2025, the MPC cut the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.25% (unanimous, neutral stance) and raised its FY26 growth forecast to 7.3%.
  • Semiconductor Mission: Construction handover of Micron’s ATMP facility in Sanand (Gujarat) was due in December 2025, with commercial production of memory modules scheduled for early 2026.
  • Global Innovation Index 2025: India ranked 38th of 139 economies in WIPO’s GII 2025, up from 39th in 2024.

🏆 Culture & Recognition

  • Lucknow — UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy: Designated on October 31, 2025 at UNESCO’s 43rd General Conference in Samarkand, making it India’s second gastronomy city after Hyderabad (2019).
