How to Score 180+ in SSC CGL Tier 1: 7 Proven Tips (2025)

Published on December 22, 2025 | For SSC CGL 2025 Aspirants

Scoring 180+ in SSC CGL Tier 1 is not just possible — it’s achievable with the right strategy. This guide shares 7 actionable tips used by toppers, based on the latest exam pattern and cut-off trends.

7 Tips to Score 180+ in SSC CGL Tier 1

1 Master the Sectional Breakdown

SSC CGL Tier 1 has 4 sections (25 Qs each): Reasoning, GK, Quant, English. To score 180+, aim for:

  • Reasoning: 45–50/50
  • Quant: 40–45/50
  • English: 40–45/50
  • GK: 35–40/50

Key: Don’t ignore GK — it’s the biggest differentiator!

2 Prioritize High-Weightage Quant Topics

70% of Quant comes from just 5 areas:

  1. Data Interpretation (usually one set of 3–4 Qs, sometimes none)
  2. Algebra (3–4 Qs)
  3. Geometry & Mensuration (4–5 Qs)
  4. Percentage, Profit & Loss (3–4 Qs)
  5. Time & Work, Speed (3–4 Qs)

→ Practice 10 DI sets daily. They’re fast and scoring.

3 Build a Current Affairs Bank

GK = 50% Static + 50% Current Affairs (last 6 months). Focus on:

  • Budget 2025 & Economic Survey highlights
  • New government schemes (PM Vishwakarma, etc.)
  • Science & Tech (ISRO missions, DPDPA, ARKA supercomputer)
  • Important appointments (CJI, CEC, Finance Secretary)

→ Revise weekly. Use our December 2025 CA summary.

4 Use the “2-Pass” Attempt Strategy

Don’t solve sequentially. Use this method:

  1. Pass 1 (40 mins): Solve all easy questions (70–80 Qs)
  2. Pass 2 (20 mins): Attempt moderate/difficult ones

This ensures you never miss easy marks due to time pressure.

5 Master the Short Reasoning Types

CGL reasoning is mostly short, one-step questions rather than long bank-style puzzles. Master these types:

  • Analogy, classification and series (letter, number and figure)
  • Coding-decoding and mathematical operations
  • Mirror and water images, paper folding, embedded figures and Venn diagrams

→ Solve 25 mixed reasoning questions daily in under 20 minutes. Speed comes with repetition.

6 Improve English with “Error Spotting”

English is the easiest to score in. Focus on:

  • Error Detection (5–7 Qs)
  • Sentence Improvement (3–4 Qs)
  • Cloze Test and Reading Comprehension (one passage each, usually 5 Qs)

→ Read 1 editorial daily (The Hindu, Indian Express).

7 Take Full-Length Mocks Weekly

Mocks build speed, accuracy, and confidence. Follow this cycle:

  1. Take a mock under exam conditions
  2. Analyze mistakes (why did you get it wrong?)
  3. Revise weak topics
  4. Repeat

Aim for 15+ mocks before exam day.

🎯 Final Tip: Practice Daily — For Free!

Consistency beats intensity. Spend just 20 minutes daily on focused MCQ practice.

🧮 What 180 Actually Requires

Each question carries 2 marks and each wrong answer costs 0.5. Here is how attempts and accuracy combine:

The lesson: 180 needs 90+ correct answers. You cannot reach it by skipping a section, and every 4 wrong answers cost you a full question's marks.

🕐 Section Order That Works for Most Toppers

  • General Awareness first (8–10 min): you either know the answer or you don't, so it is quick and builds time for later.
  • English next (12–15 min): vocabulary questions take seconds; leave long comprehension to the end of the section.
  • Reasoning (15 min): skip any question that needs more than a minute on the first pass.
  • Quant last (20+ min): it takes the most time, so it gets whatever is left.

Try at least three mocks with different orders and keep the one that gives your best score — the order matters less than sticking to it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are SSC CGL Tier 1 marks added to the final result?

No. Since the revised pattern, Tier 1 is only a screening test. It decides who reaches Tier 2; final merit comes from Tier 2.

What is the negative marking in SSC CGL Tier 1?

Each question carries 2 marks and 0.50 marks are deducted for every wrong answer. There is no penalty for unanswered questions.

How many correct answers do I need for 180 in Tier 1?

About 92 correct with 8 wrong out of 100, or 91 correct with 4 wrong if you attempt 95.
