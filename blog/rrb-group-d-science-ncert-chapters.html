RRB Group D Science: NCERT Class 6–10 Chapters You Can’t Skip

Published on December 23, 2025 | Updated October 23, 2026 | For RRB Level-1 (Group D) aspirants

In the RRB Group D (Level-1) computer based test, General Science carries 25 of the 100 questions. The other sections are Mathematics (25), General Intelligence & Reasoning (30) and General Awareness & Current Affairs (20). The test lasts 90 minutes and one-third of a mark is deducted for each wrong answer.

RRB's notifications set the science syllabus at Class 10 level, covering Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences. That is why NCERT Class 6 to 10 science books are the standard source. Always check the syllabus in the current Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) on the RRB website; the latest Level-1 CBT (CEN 09/2025) was held in August 2026.

Which NCERT edition to read

NCERT has changed its books twice in recent years. The Class 9 and 10 books were rationalised from 2023-24: some chapters were dropped and the rest renumbered. The chapter numbers below follow those rationalised editions. For Class 6 to 8, NCERT has replaced the old science books with new Curiosity textbooks, so chapter names and numbers differ by edition. Those classes are listed by topic below; older editions remain useful because Group D questions are set at Class 10 level.

Must-read chapters by subject

🔬 Physics

Class 9 (rationalised edition)

  • Ch 7 Motion
  • Ch 8 Force and Laws of Motion
  • Ch 9 Gravitation
  • Ch 10 Work and Energy
  • Ch 11 Sound

Class 10 (rationalised edition)

  • Ch 9 Light – Reflection and Refraction
  • Ch 10 The Human Eye and the Colourful World
  • Ch 11 Electricity
  • Ch 12 Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

Class 6–8 topics: measurement and units, motion and time, force and pressure, friction, heat and temperature, light and shadows, electric circuits and the effects of current, magnets, sound, and natural phenomena such as lightning and earthquakes.

🧪 Chemistry

Class 9 (rationalised edition)

  • Ch 1 Matter in Our Surroundings
  • Ch 2 Is Matter Around Us Pure?
  • Ch 3 Atoms and Molecules
  • Ch 4 Structure of the Atom

Class 10 (rationalised edition)

  • Ch 1 Chemical Reactions and Equations
  • Ch 2 Acids, Bases and Salts
  • Ch 3 Metals and Non-metals
  • Ch 4 Carbon and its Compounds

Class 6–8 topics: materials and their properties, separation of substances, physical and chemical changes, acids, bases and salts with indicators, metals and non-metals, synthetic fibres and plastics, coal and petroleum, and combustion and flame.

Dropped from the current Class 10 book but still asked in general science: periodic classification of elements (Mendeleev and the modern periodic table). Read it from an older edition or your notes.

🧬 Biology (Life Sciences)

Class 9 (rationalised edition)

  • Ch 5 The Fundamental Unit of Life
  • Ch 6 Tissues
  • Ch 12 Improvement in Food Resources

Class 10 (rationalised edition)

  • Ch 5 Life Processes
  • Ch 6 Control and Coordination
  • Ch 7 How do Organisms Reproduce?
  • Ch 8 Heredity
  • Ch 13 Our Environment

Class 6–8 topics: nutrition in plants and animals, respiration, transport in plants and animals, reproduction in plants and animals, adolescence, cell structure, microorganisms, the classification of living things, and conservation of plants and animals.

Dropped from the current Class 9 and 10 books but still common in exams: diversity in living organisms (classification), human diseases and immunity, and evolution. Cover them from older editions or a general science book.

🎯 Preparation strategy

  1. Start with Class 9 and 10: they match the stated Class 10 level; then fill gaps from Class 6–8.
  2. Make 3-column notes: Term | Definition | Example.
  3. Solve MCQs after each chapter and mark the ones you got wrong for revision.
  4. Revise diagrams and units: human eye, electric circuit, plant and animal cells, SI units and formulas.
  5. Practise past papers released by RRBs to see the level, and account for negative marking.
