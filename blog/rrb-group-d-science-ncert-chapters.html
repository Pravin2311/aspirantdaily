RRB Group D Science: NCERT Class 6–10 Chapters You Can’t Skip

Published on December 23, 2025 | For RRB Group D 2025 Aspirants

In RRB Group D, the Science section carries 25 questions (25 marks) — and 100% of them come from NCERT Class 6 to 10. You don’t need coaching or extra books. Just focus on the right chapters.

Based on analysis of 2018–2024 RRB Group D papers, here are the must-read NCERT chapters — organized by subject and class.

🎯 Preparation Strategy

  1. Read one chapter daily — 30 days = full syllabus
  2. Make 3-column notes: Term | Definition | Example

  4. Revise diagrams — Human eye, Electric circuit, Plant cell
