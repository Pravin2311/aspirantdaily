Bank Exam Syllabus 2025 (IBPS, SBI, RBI)

Last updated: October 20, 2026 | Exam patterns checked against the 2026 IBPS PO and Clerk notifications

Banking exams like IBPS PO, SBI PO, IBPS Clerk, and RBI Grade B follow a similar structure across organizations. This guide covers the Bank Exam Syllabus for both Prelims and Mains stages. The exam patterns below are taken from the IBPS notifications for CRP PO/MT-XVI (30 June 2026) and CRP CSA-XVI (Clerk, 1 August 2026); SBI and RBI publish their own patterns in each notification.

📌 Bank Exam Pattern Overview

Most bank exams have two or three stages:

  1. Preliminary Exam – Objective, qualifying
  2. Mains Exam – Objective + Descriptive (for PO)
  3. Interview – For PO/Managerial posts (IBPS Clerk has none)

Total: 100 questions | 100 marks | Sectional timing. In the 2026 PO paper, Quant carries 30 marks and Reasoning 40, so the questions are not all worth one mark. Candidates must clear the cut-off in each of the three tests.

Mains: IBPS PO (PO/MT-XVI, 2026)

PO Mains has two parts: an objective test of 170 questions for 200 marks in 160 minutes, split into four separately timed sections, and a descriptive paper of 25 marks (two questions, an essay and a comprehension, in 30 minutes, in English).

IBPS Clerk Mains (CSA-XVI) is objective only: General/Financial Awareness 40 questions for 50 marks in 20 minutes, General English 40 for 40 in 35 minutes, Reasoning Ability 40 for 60 in 35 minutes and Quantitative Aptitude 40 for 50 in 35 minutes, a total of 160 questions and 200 marks in 125 minutes.

Question counts and timings are revised from year to year, so treat the official notification as final. In both IBPS notifications a wrong answer costs 0.25 of that question's marks, and each test is separately timed.

📚 Bank Exam Syllabus 2025 – Section Wise

1. English Language

  • Reading Comprehension (1–2 passages)
  • Cloze Test, Fill in the Blanks
  • Error Detection, Sentence Correction
  • Para Jumbles, Phrase Replacement
  • Synonyms, Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases
  • (PO Mains descriptive paper) Essay and Comprehension

2. Quantitative Aptitude

  • Simplification, Approximation
  • Number Series, Quadratic Equations
  • Data Interpretation (Tables, Pie, Bar, Line)
  • Profit & Loss, SI/CI, Time & Work
  • Speed, Time & Distance, Boats & Streams
  • Probability, Permutation & Combination

3. Reasoning Ability

  • Puzzles & Seating Arrangement
  • Syllogism, Inequalities
  • Direction Sense, Blood Relations
  • Alphanumeric Series, Coding-Decoding
  • Input-Output, Logical Reasoning
  • Data Sufficiency

4. General / Banking Awareness

  • Current Affairs (Last 6 months – national & banking)
  • Banking Terms (Repo rate, CRR, SLR, NPAs)
  • Financial Awareness (latest Union Budget, Economic Survey, RBI circulars)
  • Static GK (RBI functions, NBFCs, Payment Banks)
  • Government Schemes (PMJDY, AEPS, UPI updates)

5. Computer Aptitude

Neither 2026 IBPS notification lists computer aptitude as a separate test; the PO Mains awareness test includes digital awareness. Some other bank exams test it, so check your notification.

  • Basics of Hardware & Software
  • Networking (Internet, LAN, Protocols)
  • MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Computer Abbreviations (RAM, ROM, PDF, URL)
  • Cyber Security & Recent Tech (AI, Blockchain in banking)
  • Number Systems (Binary, Decimal conversion)

🎯 Preparation Tips for Bank Exams 2025

  • ✅ Master Puzzles & DI — they dominate Reasoning & Quant
  • ✅ Read daily — The Hindu, Banking Digest, or AspirantDaily CA
  • ✅ Practice with timer — sectional timing is critical
  • ✅ Revise banking terms weekly — use flashcards
  • ✅ Take full mocks — analyze mistakes immediately

🗓️ A 12-Week Plan for Prelims

  • Weeks 1–4: Learn every arithmetic chapter and the basic reasoning topics; read one editorial daily for English.
  • Weeks 5–8: Daily puzzles (2–3 sets) and DI (2 sets); begin sectional tests with the 20-minute limit.
  • Weeks 9–12: One full mock every two days, each followed by an hour of analysis. Keep an error log and revisit it weekly.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the negative marking in bank exams?

In the IBPS PO and Clerk objective tests, one fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for each wrong answer, and unanswered questions carry no penalty. SBI states its own rule in each notification.

Is there an interview in bank clerk exams?

Not in IBPS Clerk (CRP CSA-XVI): provisional allotment is based on the Main examination, subject to the Local Language Proficiency Test. For SBI Clerk, check the SBI notification.

How many marks is the PO Mains descriptive test?

In IBPS PO (CRP PO/MT-XVI) it is 25 marks: two questions, an essay and a comprehension, in 30 minutes, in English.
