PSC Syllabus 2025 (UPPSC, MPPSC, TNPSC)

Last updated: December 21, 2025 | For State Public Service Commission Exams

State Public Service Commission (PSC) exams like UPPSC, MPPSC, TNPSC, BPSC, and KPSC recruit for state civil services. While patterns vary slightly, the core structure is similar. This guide covers the common PSC Syllabus 2025 and highlights state-specific differences.

📌 PSC Exam Pattern (Common Structure)

Most PSCs follow a three-stage process:

  1. Preliminary Exam – Objective, qualifying
  2. Mains Exam – Descriptive, merit-based
  3. Interview – Personality test

Prelims: How Three Large PSCs Compare

✅ In all three, Prelims is only a screening test: its marks do not count in the final merit list. Marking schemes, including negative marking, are set by each notification, so check yours before you plan your attempts.

📚 PSC Syllabus 2025 – Common Topics

1. General Studies (Paper I)

  • Current Affairs (National, International, State-specific)
  • History (Indian + State History & Culture)
  • Geography (World, India, and State Geography)
  • Polity (Constitution, Governance, State Government)
  • Economy (National Budget, State Economy & Schemes)
  • Environment & Ecology (National Parks, State Biodiversity)
  • Science & Tech (General + State Achievements)

2. State-Specific GK (Most Important!)

  • UPPSC: Uttar Pradesh history, rivers, districts, folk culture
  • MPPSC: Madhya Pradesh tribes, national parks, irrigation projects
  • TNPSC: Tamil Nadu Sangam literature, temples, agriculture
  • BPSC: Bihar freedom fighters, Mauryan history, Chhath Puja
  • KPSC: Karnataka dynasties, Kannada literature, sandalwood

3. CSAT (Paper II) – Qualifying

  • Comprehension
  • Interpersonal Skills
  • Logical Reasoning
  • Decision Making
  • Basic Numeracy (Class X level)
  • Data Interpretation

🎯 Preparation Tips for PSC Exams 2025

  • ✅ Master your state’s GK — state questions make up a large share of most PSC papers
  • ✅ Read state govt. portal — latest schemes, budgets, awards
  • ✅ Practice answer writing for Mains (start early!)
  • ✅ Don’t ignore CSAT — many fail to clear 33%

🗺️ How State-Specific Questions Are Actually Asked

State GK is rarely a list of facts to recite. Commissions link it to the national syllabus:

  • History: a national event with a local hero — e.g., Kunwar Singh in 1857 (BPSC), Begum Hazrat Mahal at Lucknow (UPPSC), Tantia Tope's last stand in central India (MPPSC).
  • Geography: rivers, dams and protected areas within the state — e.g., which river the Indira Sagar dam is on (Narmada, MPPSC).
  • Economy: the state budget, its flagship schemes and its rank in national reports.
  • Culture: folk dances, fairs, crafts with a GI tag, and regional literature.

📝 What Mains Usually Looks Like

PSC Mains papers are descriptive and usually include an essay, several General Studies papers and a language paper (Hindi in UPPSC and BPSC, for instance). Some states also keep an optional subject while others have dropped it, and the number of papers changes between cycles. Read the latest notification on your commission's website before choosing study material.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are State PSC Prelims marks counted in the final merit?

No. In commissions such as UPPSC, MPPSC and BPSC, Prelims is a screening test. Final merit is based on Mains and the interview.

Is CSAT qualifying in State PSC exams?

In states that have a separate aptitude paper, such as UPPSC and MPPSC, it is qualifying. BPSC has no separate aptitude paper; general mental ability questions are part of its single Prelims paper.

How should I prepare state-specific GK?

Study it alongside the national syllabus: for every history, geography or economy topic, note the state's people, places and schemes. Then practise your own commission's previous papers.
