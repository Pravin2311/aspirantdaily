Top 25 Current Affairs MCQs – December 2025 (With Explanations)

Published on December 23, 2025 | For UPSC, SSC, Bank, RRB & PSC Exams

December 2025 brought key developments in economy, science, international relations, and governance. These 25 high-yield MCQs cover the most exam-relevant events, with facts checked against NSO, RBI, MEA, WIPO, UNESCO and other official sources.

Perfect for: UPSC Prelims, SSC CGL, IBPS PO, RRB Group D, UPPSC, and other December 2025–January 2026 exams.

25 Current Affairs MCQs With Answers

1 What was India’s GDP growth rate in Q2 (July–September) of FY 2025–26, as per NSO data released on November 28, 2025?

A. 7.2%
B. 8.2%
C. 7.6%
D. 6.5%
Answer: B. 8.2%
The National Statistical Office (NSO) reported 8.2% real GDP growth for Q2 FY26 — a six-quarter high, up from 5.6% a year earlier — led by manufacturing (9.1%) and financial, real estate & professional services (10.2%).

2 Who is the current Chief of Army Staff (COAS) of India as of December 2025?

A. Manoj Pande
B. Upendra Dwivedi
C. Anil Chauhan
D. Harinder Singh
Answer: B. Upendra Dwivedi
General Upendra Dwivedi assumed office as the 30th COAS on June 30, 2024, succeeding General Manoj Pande.

3 Which Indian city was designated a UNESCO 'Creative City of Gastronomy' in October 2025?

A. Mumbai
B. Kolkata
C. Varanasi
D. Lucknow
Answer: D. Lucknow
Lucknow was named a Creative City of Gastronomy at UNESCO’s 43rd General Conference in Samarkand (October 31, 2025) for its Awadhi culinary heritage. It is India’s second city in this category after Hyderabad (2019).

4 Where is Micron Technology’s semiconductor Assembly, Test, Marking & Packaging (ATMP) facility in India located?

A. Bengaluru
B. Hyderabad
C. Sanand, Gujarat
D. Chennai
Answer: C. Sanand, Gujarat
US-based Micron Technology’s ATMP unit in Sanand, Gujarat was approved in June 2023 under the India Semiconductor Mission. Construction handover was due in December 2025, with commercial production of memory modules scheduled for early 2026.

5 The 'Arka' high-performance computing system, inaugurated in September 2024 for weather and climate research, is installed at which institute?

A. ISRO, Bengaluru
B. DRDO, Hyderabad
C. IITM, Pune
D. IIT Bombay
Answer: C. IITM, Pune
PM Modi inaugurated Arka (11.77 petaflops, Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology, Pune) and Arunika (8.24 petaflops, NCMRWF, Noida) under the Ministry of Earth Sciences in September 2024.

6 Which Finance Secretary was appointed Chairman of SEBI in February 2025?

A. T.V. Somanathan
B. Ajay Seth
C. Tuhin Kanta Pandey
D. Debasish Panda
Answer: C. Tuhin Kanta Pandey
Tuhin Kanta Pandey, designated Finance Secretary in September 2024 (and Revenue Secretary from January 2025), was named SEBI Chairman on February 27, 2025, taking charge on March 1, 2025.

7 In which city was the 18th Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (PBD) held in January 2025?

A. Varanasi
B. Bhubaneswar
C. Indore
D. Ahmedabad
Answer: B. Bhubaneswar
The 18th PBD was held in Bhubaneswar, Odisha from January 8–10, 2025, themed “Diaspora’s Contribution to a Viksit Bharat”. (Varanasi hosted the 16th PBD in 2019.)

8 Which country won the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup 2024?

A. South Africa
B. India
C. Australia
D. England
Answer: B. India
India defeated South Africa in the final on June 29, 2024, in Barbados to win their second T20 World Cup.

9 What is India’s target year for achieving 'Net Zero' emissions as per its updated NDC?

A. 2050
B. 2060
C. 2070
D. 2047
Answer: C. 2070
India announced its 2070 net zero target at COP26 (Glasgow, 2021) and included it in its updated NDC and long-term strategy.

10 Who was elected President of the European Council in June 2024?

A. Ursula von der Leyen
B. António Costa
C. Charles Michel
D. Josep Borrell
Answer: B. António Costa
Former Portuguese Prime Minister António Costa was elected President of the European Council on June 27, 2024 and took office on December 1, 2024, succeeding Charles Michel.

11 Which Indian state launched the 'Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana' in July 2024?

A. Maharashtra
B. Madhya Pradesh
C. Chhattisgarh
D. Rajasthan
Answer: A. Maharashtra
Approved by the Maharashtra cabinet on June 28, 2024 and launched in July 2024, the scheme provides ₹1,500 monthly via DBT to women aged 21–65 with family income up to ₹2.5 lakh a year.

12 Which country hosted the G20 Summit in 2024?

A. India
B. Brazil
C. South Africa
D. Indonesia
Answer: B. Brazil
Brazil hosted the G20 Summit in Rio de Janeiro in November 2024 under its presidency.

13 Which Indian state became the first to pass a 'Right to Health' law, in March 2023?

A. Kerala
B. Rajasthan
C. Delhi
D. Odisha
Answer: B. Rajasthan
The Rajasthan Assembly passed the Right to Health Bill on March 21, 2023 (Governor’s assent April 12, 2023). It bars public and specified private hospitals from refusing emergency treatment.

14 Who was appointed the 51st Chief Justice of India in November 2024?

A. D.Y. Chandrachud
B. Sanjiv Khanna
C. B.R. Gavai
D. Surya Kant
Answer: B. Sanjiv Khanna
Justice Sanjiv Khanna assumed office as CJI on November 11, 2024, succeeding Justice D.Y. Chandrachud.

15 To what level did the RBI cut the repo rate in its monetary policy of December 5, 2025?

A. 5.25%
B. 5.50%
C. 5.00%
D. 5.75%
Answer: A. 5.25%
The MPC voted 6–0 to cut the repo rate by 25 bps to 5.25%, keeping a neutral stance. SDF moved to 5.00% and MSF to 5.50%; the FY26 growth forecast was raised to 7.3%.

16 Where was the 2024 BRICS Summit held?

A. Moscow
B. Kazan
C. St. Petersburg
D. Sochi
Answer: B. Kazan
The 2024 BRICS Summit was held in Kazan, Russia, in October 2024 under Russia’s presidency.

17 Which Indian state launched the 'Chief Minister’s Breakfast Scheme' for school students in September 2022 and extended it to urban aided schools in August 2025?

A. Tamil Nadu
B. Kerala
C. Andhra Pradesh
D. Karnataka
Answer: A. Tamil Nadu
Tamil Nadu launched the scheme on September 15, 2022 for 1.14 lakh students in Classes 1–5. After expansions in 2023, 2024 and August 2025, it covers about 20 lakh children.

18 Which startup launched the Agnibaan SOrTeD rocket from India’s first private launchpad, ‘Dhanush’, in May 2024?

A. Agnikul Cosmos
B. Skyroot Aerospace
C. Pixxel
D. Dhruva Space
Answer: A. Agnikul Cosmos
Agnikul’s sub-orbital flight from Sriharikota on May 30, 2024 used the world’s first single-piece 3D-printed semi-cryogenic engine. It was India’s second privately built rocket after Skyroot’s Vikram-S (2022).

19 Which body operates the 'Namo Bharat' trains on the Delhi–Meerut RRTS corridor, inaugurated in October 2023?

A. Ministry of Railways
B. NCRTC
C. Ministry of Urban Development
D. DMRC
Answer: B. NCRTC
PM Modi inaugurated the 17-km Sahibabad–Duhai Depot priority section on October 20, 2023. The corridor is built and run by the National Capital Region Transport Corporation (NCRTC).

20 What was India’s rank in WIPO’s Global Innovation Index (GII) 2025?

A. 38th
B. 39th
C. 40th
D. 36th
Answer: A. 38th
India ranked 38th of 139 economies in GII 2025 (up from 39th in 2024 and 48th in 2020), topping the lower-middle-income group. Switzerland ranked first.

21 Which organization released the 'World Economic Outlook' in October 2024?

A. World Bank
B. IMF
C. WTO
D. UNDP
Answer: B. IMF
The International Monetary Fund (IMF) releases the World Economic Outlook biannually (April & October).

22 Which edition of the India–Russia Annual Summit was held in New Delhi on December 4–5, 2025?

A. 21st
B. 22nd
C. 23rd
D. 24th
Answer: C. 23rd
President Vladimir Putin made a state visit to India for the 23rd Annual Summit with PM Modi — his first visit since the 21st summit in December 2021.

23 Who won the Nobel Peace Prize 2024?

A. Volodymyr Zelenskyy
B. Nihon Hidankyo
C. Maria Ressa
D. Not awarded
Answer: B. Nihon Hidankyo
The 2024 Nobel Peace Prize was awarded to Nihon Hidankyo, a Japanese organization of atomic bomb survivors.

24 What is the name of India’s first indigenously developed mRNA vaccine, which received emergency use authorisation in June 2022?

A. Covaxin
B. ZyCoV-D
C. GEMCOVAC-19
D. Corbevax
Answer: C. GEMCOVAC-19
Developed by Pune-based Gennova Biopharmaceuticals (an Emcure subsidiary), GEMCOVAC-19 received DCGI emergency use authorisation on June 28, 2022. Unlike most mRNA vaccines, it can be stored at 2–8°C.

25 Which city hosted the India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2024?

A. Bengaluru
B. Hyderabad
C. New Delhi
D. Mumbai
Answer: C. New Delhi
IMC 2024 was held in New Delhi from October 15–18, 2024, focusing on 5G, AI, and semiconductor ecosystems.
