10 High-Frequency Polity Topics for UPSC Prelims: Practice MCQs

Published on December 23, 2025 | Updated October 20, 2026

Polity questions in UPSC Prelims keep returning to the same core ideas: the basic structure doctrine, constitutional remedies, federal features, Fundamental Duties and constitutional bodies. UPSC rarely repeats a question word for word, but if you understand these concepts you can handle the many ways they are framed.

These are practice questions written by AspirantDaily on topics covered in standard references such as M. Laxmikanth’s Indian Polity and the Constitution itself. They are not reproductions of actual UPSC questions. To practise with real papers, download UPSC’s previous question papers from upsc.gov.in.

10 Practice Questions on High-Frequency Polity Topics

1 Which of the following is/are features of Indian federalism?

1. Dual polity
2. Written Constitution
3. Independent judiciary
4. Bicameralism
Answer: All of the above (1, 2, 3, 4)
Indian federalism includes all these features, though it’s “quasi-federal” due to strong Centre.

2 The ‘Basic Structure Doctrine’ was propounded by the Supreme Court in which case?

A. Golaknath Case
B. Kesavananda Bharati Case
C. Minerva Mills Case
D. S.R. Bommai Case
Answer: B. Kesavananda Bharati Case (1973)
This landmark judgment held that Parliament cannot alter the “basic structure” of the Constitution.

3 Which Article of the Constitution provides for the Finance Commission?

A. Article 260
B. Article 270
C. Article 280
D. Article 290
Answer: C. Article 280
Article 280 empowers the President to constitute a Finance Commission every 5 years.

4 The ‘Doctrine of Pith and Substance’ is used to determine:

A. The validity of a law
B. The residuary powers of Parliament
C. The true nature of legislation
D. The scope of Fundamental Rights
Answer: C. The true nature of legislation
Used when a law appears to encroach on another list — court examines its “pith and substance”.

5 Which Schedule of the Constitution deals with the allocation of seats in the Rajya Sabha?

A. Third Schedule
B. Fourth Schedule
C. Fifth Schedule
D. Sixth Schedule
Answer: B. Fourth Schedule
Fourth Schedule specifies Rajya Sabha seats per state. Third = Oaths, Fifth = Scheduled Areas.

6 The ‘Right to Constitutional Remedies’ is enshrined in which Article?

A. Article 32
B. Article 226
C. Article 323B
D. Article 356
Answer: A. Article 32
Dr. Ambedkar called Article 32 the “heart and soul” of the Constitution.

7 Which of the following is NOT a Fundamental Duty?

A. To abide by the Constitution
B. To protect public property
C. To pay taxes
D. To develop scientific temper
Answer: C. To pay taxes
Paying taxes is a legal duty, not a Fundamental Duty (Part IVA, Article 51A).

8 The ‘Special Status’ of Jammu and Kashmir was granted under which Article?

A. Article 356
B. Article 370
C. Article 35A
D. Both B and C
Answer: B. Article 370
Article 370 granted special status. Article 35A was inserted by the Constitution (Application to Jammu and Kashmir) Order, 1954, a Presidential Order issued under Article 370, and let the state legislature define “permanent residents”. Both ceased to operate after the Presidential Order of August 5, 2019.

9 Which body is responsible for conducting elections to Panchayats?

A. Election Commission of India
B. State Election Commission
C. State Government
D. Union Ministry of Panchayati Raj
Answer: B. State Election Commission
Article 243K mandates State Election Commissions for Panchayat and Municipal elections.

10 The ‘Doctrine of Severability’ implies that:

A. Only the invalid part of a law is struck down
B. The entire law is void if one part is invalid
C. Laws can be severed by the President
D. State laws are severed from Central laws
Answer: A. Only the invalid part of a law is struck down
If a law has valid and invalid parts, only the invalid portion is removed (Article 13).

🎯 How to Use This List

  • ✅ Learn the concept, not the wording — the same idea can be tested through statements, pairs or assertion–reason formats.
  • ✅ Understand the concept behind each — UPSC may rephrase the options.
  • ✅ Revise weekly — add them to your “must-know” flashcards.
