RRB Syllabus 2026 (NTPC, Group D, JE)

Last updated: October 28, 2026 | Pattern from the official RRB notifications

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) exams like NTPC, Group D, and JE are among India’s largest government recruitment drives. This guide gives the RRB syllabus and exam pattern from the current official notifications: CEN 06/2025 (NTPC Graduate, October 2025), CEN 09/2025 (Level 1, the old Group D, January 2026) and CEN 04/2026 (Junior Engineer, August 2026).

📌 RRB Exam Pattern Overview

RRB exams are conducted in multiple stages depending on the post:

  • RRB NTPC (Graduate): CBT 1 → CBT 2 → Typing Skill Test (clerk-typist posts) or Aptitude Test (Station Master, Traffic Assistant) → Document Verification → Medical
  • RRB Group D (Level 1): CBT → PET → Document Verification → Medical
  • RRB JE (Junior Engineer and DMS): CBT 1 → CBT 2 → Document Verification → Medical

Each notification says its section-wise split is indicative, so the actual paper can vary slightly.

📚 RRB Syllabus 2026 – Section Wise

1. Mathematics

  • Number System, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF
  • Ratio & Proportion, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest
  • Time & Work, Time & Distance, Mensuration
  • Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics
  • Group D and JE also list BODMAS, Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar & Clock, Pipes & Cistern

2. General Intelligence & Reasoning

  • Analogies, Alphabetical & Number Series, Coding-Decoding
  • Mathematical Operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagrams
  • Similarities & Differences, Analytical Reasoning, Decision Making
  • NTPC also lists Puzzle, Data Sufficiency, Statement–Conclusion, Statement–Courses of Action, Maps, Interpretation of Graphs
  • Group D and JE also list Data Interpretation & Sufficiency, Classification, Directions, Statement–Arguments & Assumptions

3. General Science

  • Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at Class 10 (CBSE) level for Group D and JE CBT 1
  • NTPC asks General Science and Life Science up to Class 10 CBSE inside General Awareness
  • Chapter list: RRB Group D Science: NCERT Chapters

4. General Awareness & Current Affairs

  • Current events of national and international importance, games and sports
  • History, geography, polity and economy of India, including the freedom struggle
  • Science and technology developments, including India's space and nuclear programmes
  • NTPC also lists art and culture, monuments, transport systems in India, flagship government programmes, the UN and other world organisations, basics of computers and common abbreviations

🎯 Preparation Tips for RRB Exams 2026

  • ✅ Start with NCERT Class 9–10 Science — General Science is 25 of 100 Group D questions and 30 of 100 in JE CBT 1
  • ✅ Practice 30+ Maths MCQs daily — speed is key
  • ✅ Revise Railway GK — the NTPC syllabus lists transport systems in India
  • ✅ Take timed mocks — 100 questions in 90 minutes, and each wrong answer costs a third of a mark

📈 RRB NTPC CBT 2 Pattern

Candidates shortlisted from CBT 1 take a second CBT, whose normalised marks decide the merit list:

SectionQuestions
General Awareness50
Mathematics35
General Intelligence & Reasoning35
Total120 questions in 90 minutes

Senior Clerk cum Typist and Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist then need a qualifying typing skill test (30 words per minute in English or 25 in Hindi). Station Master and Traffic Assistant need a computer-based aptitude test with no negative marking; their merit is 70% CBT 2 and 30% aptitude test. CBT 2 deducts one-third of a mark for each wrong answer, and scores across shifts are normalised.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in RRB exams?

Yes. The NTPC (CEN 06/2025), Group D (CEN 09/2025) and JE (CEN 04/2026) notifications all deduct one-third of a mark for each wrong answer. Unanswered questions lose nothing. The aptitude test (CBAT) for Station Master and Traffic Assistant has no negative marking.

What is the RRB Group D PET standard?

Under CEN 09/2025, men must carry 35 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes and run 1,000 m in 4 minutes 15 seconds. Women must carry 20 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes and run 1,000 m in 5 minutes 40 seconds. The PET is qualifying.

How many questions are in RRB NTPC CBT 2?

120 questions in 90 minutes: 50 General Awareness, 35 Mathematics and 35 General Intelligence & Reasoning.

What are the minimum qualifying marks in RRB CBTs?

UR and EWS 40%, OBC (non-creamy layer) 30% and SC 30% in all three notifications. ST is 25% for NTPC and JE and 30% for Group D. PwBD candidates can get a 2-mark relaxation if there are not enough PwBD candidates.
