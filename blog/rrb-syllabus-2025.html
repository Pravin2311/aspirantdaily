RRB Syllabus 2025 (NTPC, Group D, JE)

Last updated: December 21, 2025 | Official Syllabus for Railway Exams

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) exams like NTPC, Group D, and JE are among India’s largest government recruitment drives. This guide covers the complete RRB Syllabus 2025 for all major posts, based on the latest official notifications from Indian Railways.

📌 RRB Exam Pattern Overview

RRB exams are conducted in multiple stages depending on the post:

  • RRB NTPC (Graduate): CBT 1 → CBT 2 → Typing Test → Document Verification
  • RRB Group D (10th Pass): CBT → PET → Document Verification
  • RRB JE (Diploma/Engineering): CBT 1 → CBT 2 → Document Verification

📚 RRB Syllabus 2025 – Section Wise

1. Mathematics

  • Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions
  • LCM, HCF, Ratio & Proportion
  • Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest
  • Time & Work, Time & Distance, Speed
  • Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration
  • Elementary Statistics, Square Root

2. General Intelligence & Reasoning

  • Analogies, Alphabetical & Number Series
  • Coding-Decoding, Mathematical Operations
  • Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling
  • Venn Diagrams, Data Interpretation
  • Conclusions & Decision Making
  • Similarities & Differences, Analytical Reasoning

3. General Science

  • Physics: Motion, Force, Work, Energy, Sound, Light
  • Chemistry: Reactions, Metals, Non-metals, Acids, Bases
  • Biology: Human Body, Nutrition, Diseases, Environment
  • Basic concepts up to Class 10 level

4. General Awareness & Current Affairs

  • Current Events (National & International, last 6 months)
  • Indian Railways (History, Zones, Recent Initiatives)
  • Static GK (Books, Awards, Sports, Important Days)
  • Polity, Geography, Economy (Basic level)

🎯 Preparation Tips for RRB Exams 2025

  • ✅ Focus on NCERT Science (Class 6–10) — 25% of Group D paper
  • ✅ Practice 30+ Maths MCQs daily — speed is key
  • ✅ Revise Railway GK — zones, ministers, recent projects
  • ✅ Take timed mocks — 90 mins for 100 questions is intense!

📈 RRB NTPC CBT 2 Pattern

Candidates who clear CBT 1 take a tougher second CBT, which decides merit for most posts:

SectionQuestions
General Awareness50
Mathematics35
General Intelligence & Reasoning35
Total120 questions in 90 minutes

Posts such as clerk-cum-typist and accounts clerk then need a typing skill test (30 words per minute in English or 25 in Hindi), while station master and traffic assistant posts need a computer-based aptitude test. All RRB CBTs deduct one-third of a mark for each wrong answer, and scores across shifts are normalised.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in RRB exams?

Yes. RRB computer-based tests deduct one-third of the marks for each wrong answer. Unanswered questions get no penalty.

What is the RRB Group D PET standard?

Men must carry 35 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes and run 1,000 m in 4 minutes 15 seconds. Women must carry 20 kg for 100 m in 2 minutes and run 1,000 m in 5 minutes 40 seconds. Check your notification for any changes.

How many questions are in RRB NTPC CBT 2?

120 questions in 90 minutes: 50 General Awareness, 35 Mathematics and 35 General Intelligence & Reasoning.
